Praxis · before the frame · src/cook/mesh.rs

Mesh cooking

The cook of one Mesh: each Primitive of a glTF file or a procedural scene welded on position, UV and normal, given MikkTSpace tangents, packed as 28-byte vertices with a QTangent, ordered for the vertex cache, overdraw and vertex fetch and cut in order into Meshlets; then the whole Mesh simplified, each LOD to half the triangles of the one before, on LOD 0's vertices, with every Thin Primitive kept whole and its radius stored; all of it in one Mesh Blob, the same bytes on every machine that cooks it.

Describes main at 53708600 (2026-11-21). A rewrite of this page moves the commit; nothing is appended. Read first: Texture cooking for the Cook key and the Cooked cache, Camera-relative placement for the 128 m a vertex may lie from its pivot, The culling pass for how a LOD is chosen from its LOD error.

Interface

Runs
Before the frame, never during it: in praxis cook, one Mesh after another on the calling thread, for each Mesh of a manifest source or a Reference scene whose Cook key changed; in a dev launch, for a Mesh a load names whose Blob is missing or stale; and, its LOD 0 and LOD steps only, inside the Far proxy cook.
Reads
A Mesh's Primitives as SourcePrimitives: a Geometry of positions, normals, UVs and indices, a box its bounds must hold, if any, a Thin Primitive's radius, if any, and the Asset ID of its Material. From glTF through gltf_meshes, from a procedural scene through its meshes.
Writes
One CookedMesh per Mesh, stored as a Blob of magic PXME and MESH_FORMAT_VERSION 8 into the Cooked cache, under its Asset ID and Cook key.
Counted
Not in Frame stats: the cook runs outside the frame. The cook's cook complete log counts Meshes among the Assets cooked and unchanged; the frame later counts what a loaded Mesh takes of the arenas and the triangles it draws.
Where in the code
cook, lod_0, coarser_lods, push_lod, cook_primitive, weld, qtangent, meshlet_triangle_counts, thin_primitive, cook_key and gltf_meshes in src/cook/mesh.rs; mesh_blob and cook_sources in src/cook/mod.rs; the Blob in CookedMesh, CookedLod, CookedPrimitive, CookedMeshlet and CookedVertex, src/assets/cooked.rs; Geometry and Geometry::thin in src/scenes/mod.rs; the compiler flag in .cargo/config.toml.

1 · gltf_meshes · Geometry

What the cook reads

Every Mesh reaches the cook in one shape, whatever its source: a list of Primitives, each an indexed triangle list in mesh-local space with the Material it names. A Primitive is declared thin at its source, by a radius in metres, and the cook takes that radius as it is.

gltf_meshes reads each Mesh of a glTF file: triangles only, positions and normals required, the first UV set or zeros, indices or the vertices in order, and the Material by the Asset ID the Material cook gives it. A procedural scene's meshes builds the same Geometry in code, and a scene's Material is named by the scene's own Asset ID. Either may declare a Primitive thin. In glTF it is a number named thin_radius_metres in the Primitive's extras; a value there that is not a number stops the cook with the Mesh's name.

let thin_radius_metres = (primitive.extras().as_deref())
    .map(|extras| serde_json::from_str::<serde_json::Value>(extras.get()))
    .transpose()?
    .as_ref()
    .and_then(|extras| extras.get("thin_radius_metres"))
    .map(|radius_metres| {
        radius_metres.as_f64().with_context(|| {
            format!("{name:?}: a primitive's thin_radius_metres is {radius_metres}")
        })
    })
    .transpose()?
    .map(|radius_metres| radius_metres as f32);

gltf_meshes, src/cook/mesh.rs.

In code a scene calls Geometry::thin. town declares its wires at 1.5 cm, its railing posts, top rails and aerial masts at 2.5 cm, the aerial's bars at 1.5 cm, its lamps at 7.5 and 4 cm and its utility poles at 11.5 and 7 cm, the aerial, the lamp and the pole as two Primitives each. A Primitive may also carry a box its Mesh's bounds must hold besides its vertices: a bent Mesh's every shape, or a Far proxy's Chunk.

2 · cook · thin_primitive

What it refuses

A Mesh with a vertex more than 128 m from its pivot is refused, and so is a Primitive declared thin that is not a tube of about its radius. Either stops the cook with a message naming the Mesh.

anyhow::ensure!(
    farthest_metres <= MAX_PIVOT_DISTANCE_METRES,
    "a vertex lies {farthest_metres:.1} m from the Mesh's pivot, past ADR 0014's {MAX_PIVOT_DISTANCE_METRES} m"
);

cook, src/cook/mesh.rs. The Far proxy cook calls lod_0 directly and is not held to it.

thin_primitive checks a declared tube against its normals. An edge whose two ends, each moved inwards along its normal by the radius, land within a quarter of the radius times the normals' difference of each other lies on one ring of the tube; a triangle passes if all its corners lie at the positions of ring vertices and it has such an edge, a side, or every edge no longer than the ring's diameter widened by a quarter, a cap. Any other triangle is refused with its corners. A box declared thin fails, as its faces' normals do not point away from a centre line; a cylinder of six faces at 5 cm cooks declared at 6 cm, not at 7.

Passed, the Primitive's cap vertices take the normal of the ring vertex at the same position, so that the cap's rim moves out with the tube's side when the vertex shader widens the tube to a pixel, and nothing opens between them.

let mut tube = geometry.clone();
for (vertex, normal) in (0..).zip(&mut tube.normals) {
    if !ring_vertices.contains(&vertex)
        && let Some(ring_normal) = ring_normals.get(&position_bits(vertex))
    {
        *normal = *ring_normal;
    }
}

thin_primitive, src/cook/mesh.rs.

3 · cook_primitive · weld · qtangent

Weld, tangents, QTangent

A Primitive's vertices are welded where position, UV and normal are equal to the bit, given a MikkTSpace tangent per corner, and split again only where two corners of one vertex end up with different tangent frames. Each vertex is then 28 bytes: position and UV in f32 and the whole tangent frame as one quaternion in four 16-bit numbers.

weld keys each vertex by its eight floats and merges equal keys with meshoptimizer's vertex remap. corner_tangents runs bevy_mikktspace over the welded triangles, which glTF prescribes for a Primitive that brings no tangents, with v flipped, since MikkTSpace puts the bitangent along +v and glTF's v runs down the image while its normal Textures take +Y as up. Each corner then becomes a CookedVertex, and the corners are welded again, on all 28 bytes, so a vertex whose corners disagree on their tangent stays split there.

// Corners of one welded vertex stay apart where their QTangents differ.
let (vertex_count, remap) = meshopt::generate_vertex_remap(&corners, None);
let vertices = meshopt::remap_vertex_buffer(&corners, vertex_count, &remap);
let indices = meshopt::remap_index_buffer(None, corners.len(), &remap);

cook_primitive, src/cook/mesh.rs.

qtangent builds the frame whose columns are the tangent made perpendicular to the normal, the bitangent and the normal, takes its rotation as a unit quaternion with w made positive, and then gives w the bitangent sign: a quaternion and its negation are the same rotation, so the sign is free to carry it. A w that would round to zero is held at one Snorm16 step, QTANGENT_SMALLEST_W, so that the sign survives.

if rotation.w < QTANGENT_SMALLEST_W {
    let axis =
        rotation.xyz().normalize() * (1.0 - QTANGENT_SMALLEST_W * QTANGENT_SMALLEST_W).sqrt();
    rotation = Quat::from_xyzw(axis.x, axis.y, axis.z, QTANGENT_SMALLEST_W);
}
if bitangent_sign < 0.0 {
    rotation = -rotation;
}
rotation
    .to_array()
    .map(|component| (component * i16::MAX as f32).round() as i16)

qtangent, src/cook/mesh.rs.

Each Primitive also gets its texel density, texel_density: the square root of its triangles' UV area over their area in metres, which mip priority reads later as UV units per metre.

4 · lod_0 · meshlet_triangle_counts · CookedMeshlet::split

Order and Meshlets

Each Primitive's triangles are ordered for the vertex cache and then for overdraw, and cut, in that order, into the longest runs that keep to 64 vertices and 124 triangles. The Meshlets follow the index stream and never reorder it, so a cook changes no image by cutting them.

cook_primitive ends with meshoptimizer's vertex cache pass and its overdraw pass, which may worsen the cache order by up to OVERDRAW_THRESHOLD, 1.05. lod_0 appends the Primitives' vertices and indices one after another, renumbers the whole Mesh's vertices in order of first use for vertex fetch, which keeps each Primitive's vertices one run, and then cuts each Primitive. meshlet_triangle_counts walks the triangles and opens a new Meshlet when the next would pass either limit, as meshoptimizer's scan builder does; CookedMeshlet::split then gives each Meshlet its vertices, numbered in order of first use with each triangle's corners taken lowest first, so that the numbering survives the index codec rotating a triangle.

if counts
    .last()
    .is_none_or(|&count| count == MESHLET_MAX_TRIANGLES)
    || run_vertices.len() + new_vertices > MESHLET_MAX_VERTICES
{
    for vertex in run_vertices.drain(..) {
        in_run[vertex as usize] = false;
    }
    counts.push(0);
}

meshlet_triangle_counts, src/cook/mesh.rs. MESHLET_MAX_VERTICES is 64, as a packed index carries the vertex within its Meshlet in six bits; MESHLET_MAX_TRIANGLES is 124, what a closed mesh of 64 vertices has, so the vertex limit binds first on closed geometry.

The Mesh's bounds are the box around its vertices and any box a Primitive brings.

5 · coarser_lods

The LOD chain

Each coarser LOD is the one before simplified to half its triangles, the whole Mesh in one call, with normals and UVs weighed beside positions and no error limit. The chain ends at eight LODs, at a LOD that keeps more than 85% of the one before, or at an empty one. Every LOD indexes LOD 0's vertices; only its indices and Meshlets are its own.

coarser_lods hands meshoptimizer the Mesh's 28-byte vertices for positions and, per vertex, five attributes: the normal decoded from its QTangent, weighted LOD_NORMAL_WEIGHT, 0.5, as the simplifier's own example weighs normals, and the UV divided by the UV units across the Mesh's extent, weighted LOD_UV_WEIGHT, 1. The target is half the indices of the finer LOD, the error limit f32::MAX.

let simplified = meshopt::simplify_with_attributes_and_locks(
    &finer,
    &positions,
    attributes.as_flattened(),
    &weights,
    size_of::<[f32; 5]>(),
    &locked,
    finer.len() / 6 * 3,
    // No error limit [ref:meshoptimizer-0.25-simplify-error].
    f32::MAX,
    meshopt::SimplifyOptions::None,
    Some(&mut relative_error),
);
if simplified.is_empty()
    || simplified.len() as f32 > finer.len() as f32 * LOD_KEPT_INDICES_CEILING
{
    break;
}
error_metres += relative_error * extent_metres;

coarser_lods, src/cook/mesh.rs.

The simplifier reports its error relative to the Mesh's extent; times simplify_scale it is metres, and each LOD's LOD error is the sum of those down to it, as meshoptimizer advises for a chain each of whose levels starts from the one before. The error mixes position, normal and UV, so it is a measure of its own rather than a distance; the culling pass compares it with a pixel's size at the Instance (The culling pass).

Simplifying the whole Mesh in one call collapses a border between two Primitives the same way on both sides, so no crack opens there. The simplifier keeps each triangle on vertices of one Primitive, since no two Primitives share a vertex; the cook splits the result back by Primitive, refuses a triangle that mixes two, and orders and cuts each Primitive's run as LOD 0's. A Primitive a LOD leaves no triangle of keeps an empty run, with its Material.

push_lod is the other way into a chain, for a cook that simplifies its own geometry: it cooks each Primitive of the new LOD as LOD 0's were and appends only the vertices no earlier LOD holds in the same Primitive, found by their 28 bytes. The Far proxy cook uses it; a source Mesh never does.

6 · coarser_lods · CookedPrimitive

Thin Primitives

A Thin Primitive's vertices are locked, so every LOD keeps its whole tube and the halving falls on the Mesh's other Primitives. Its radius is stored with the Primitive in every LOD, where the main pass reads it to widen the tube to a pixel.

let locked: Vec<bool> = primitive_of_vertex
    .iter()
    .map(|&primitive_index| {
        mesh.primitives[primitive_index]
            .thin_radius_metres
            .is_some()
    })
    .collect();

coarser_lods, src/cook/mesh.rs.

Collapsed, a tube of six faces would turn into a ribbon or a line of no width, which the vertex shader cannot widen along its normals. Locked, it stays a tube: a Mesh whose Primitives are all thin, as each of town's wires, posts, rails, aerials, lamps and poles is, cannot halve, keeps more than 85% of its triangles at the first try and has a chain of one LOD. A Mesh with a thin Primitive beside others, a post beside a sphere in the tests, halves the sphere and keeps the post at every LOD.

CookedPrimitive holds the radius as thin_radius_metres; a vertex's position less its normal times the radius lies on the tube's centre line, which is what the widening needs. The Blob stores zero for a Primitive that is not thin.

7 · qtangent_normal · .cargo/config.toml

The same bytes on every machine

A Mesh Blob is the same bytes on the M4 Pro and on CI's Linux runners. The cook does the arithmetic whose rounding varies by architecture in scalar code, and meshoptimizer's C++ is built without fused multiply-adds; two hash tests fail on a machine that cooks other bytes.

glam normalises a quaternion and takes its dot products differently on NEON and on SSE2, so qtangent normalises by hand and qtangent_normal, which decodes the normal the simplifier weighs, is scalar.

pub(crate) fn qtangent_normal(qtangent: [i16; 4]) -> [f32; 3] {
    // Scalar only: glam's SIMD rounds by architecture, and a Blob's bytes must not.
    let [x, y, z, w] = qtangent.map(f32::from);
    let length_squared = x * x + y * y + z * z + w * w;
    [
        2.0 * (x * z + w * y) / length_squared,
        2.0 * (y * z - w * x) / length_squared,
        1.0 - 2.0 * (x * x + y * y) / length_squared,
    ]
}

qtangent_normal, src/cook/mesh.rs.

A compiler that fuses a multiply and an add rounds once where meshoptimizer rounds twice, and the simplifier then collapses other edges. The meshopt crate builds meshoptimizer through cc, which reads the flags .cargo/config.toml sets for the two targets that cook:

# meshoptimizer must not fuse a multiply and an add: a compiler that does cooks other bytes, and a Blob is the same bytes on every machine.
CXXFLAGS_aarch64_apple_darwin = "-ffp-contract=off"
CXXFLAGS_x86_64_unknown_linux_gnu = "-ffp-contract=off"

.cargo/config.toml.

The procedural scenes' geometry is part of the input: a sphere's sines and cosines are taken in f64 and rounded, because the platforms' f32 sine and cosine differ. the_reference_scenes_meshes_cook_to_the_same_blobs_on_every_machine hashes every Reference scene's Mesh Blobs and the committed glTF's; the_densest_chunks_proxy_cooks_to_the_same_blob_on_every_machine hashes a Far proxy. A hash that moves on every machine is a change to the cook; one that moves on a single machine has met one of these roundings.

8 · CookedMesh::to_blob · cook_key

The Blob and its Cook key

A Mesh Blob is the magic PXME, its format version and one zstd frame at level 19: counts, bounds, the Primitives, each LOD's table, a byte per Meshlet, then meshoptimizer's encoded vertices and every LOD's indices as one encoded stream. A Meshlet stores only its triangle count; its vertices and triangles are rebuilt from its run of the indices on reading.

FieldBytes eachEncoding
vertex, Primitive and LOD counts3 × 4u32, little-endian
bounds, lowest then highest6 × 4f32
per Primitive: Material Asset ID, texel density, Thin Primitive radius16, 4, 4u128 big-endian, zero for none; f32; f32, zero for none
per LOD, LOD 0 first: LOD error, index count4, 4f32 metres; u32
per LOD and Primitive: first index, index count, Meshlet count3 × 4u32
per Meshlet, every LOD's in order: triangle count1u8
encoded vertices: their byte length, then the stream4 + …meshoptimizer's vertex codec 0
every LOD's indices…meshoptimizer's index codec, one stream

CookedMesh::from_blob refuses another magic or version with a re-cook message, a LOD count outside 1 to MESH_MAX_LODS, a LOD error that is not finite or is smaller than the finer LOD's, an index count not whole triangles, a Primitive past its LOD's indices, a negative radius, and a Meshlet past its limits. CookedCache::mesh reads one by Asset ID.

The Cook key is SHA-256 over the magic, the version, a sentence naming every encoder version and setting, then every field of each Primitive's Geometry and its Material, each length-prefixed. The fields are taken by name, so a field added to Geometry does not compile until the key covers it.

let Geometry {
    positions,
    normals,
    uvs,
    indices,
    bounds,
    thin_radius_metres,
} = geometry;

cook_key, src/cook/mesh.rs. encoder_settings writes the sentence: bevy_mikktspace's and meshoptimizer's versions, the vertex codec, the overdraw threshold, the Meshlet limits, the chain's rules and weights, and zstd's version and level.

The key does not see the compiler's flags or how the cook rounds; when those changed, MESH_FORMAT_VERSION, which every key covers, was bumped, and every Cooked cache cooked its Meshes once more.

11 · ADRs 0014, 0018, 0019, 0027

Why this way

A discrete chain, cooked. A chain of simplified LODs, picked per Instance by projected error, is what shipped engines cook; ADR 0019 rules out virtual geometry beside 4x MSAA, as Nanite and Bevy's meshlets draw without MSAA and Nanite hides its switches with TAA. The LOD survey (docs/research/lod-hlod-and-far-field.md) estimated that drawing half of town's indices takes about a third off its gate.

LOD 0's vertices, not each LOD's own. Unique vertices per LOD would let a coarse LOD move its vertices to fit better, at about twice the vertex memory; the simplifier's default reuses the source's, the survey's first option, and the cook takes it. Only the Far proxy cook, whose faces need normals of their own, appends vertices.

Each LOD from the one before, its error summed. Simplifying each LOD from LOD 0 is the other way meshoptimizer names; starting from the previous LOD is more efficient and gives smoother transitions between LODs, and its errors are then accumulated for selection.

The whole Mesh in one call. Simplified Primitive by Primitive, two Primitives sharing a border would each collapse it their own way and open a crack between them; in one call both sides collapse the same edges.

Meshlets in index order. ADR 0018 cuts each Primitive as meshoptimizer's scan builder does. Its spatial builder reorders the triangles, the index codec then rotates them differently, and images changed; in order, the vertex cache and overdraw passes' order is the drawn order.

A 28-byte vertex with a QTangent. ADR 0018 packs the normal, tangent and bitangent sign into one quaternion against the 48 bytes before; positions stay f32, as 16 bits over a 256 m pivot range would be 3.9 mm, past ADR 0014's bar.

Thin Primitives declared, not found. ADR 0027 widens a Thin Primitive's vertices from its centre line until it draws a pixel wide, which needs a radius the cook can check against the normals. Declared at the source, the radius is the author's, and the cook only refuses a shape that is not the tube it claims; locking it keeps that tube in every LOD, where a collapsed one could not be widened.

Bytes fixed by the source. A Blob is named by its content hash, so two machines that round differently cook two Blobs for one source and tests that compare against one of them fail on the other. Scalar arithmetic and an unfused build make the bytes a property of the source alone.

12

Limits

The error is the simplifier's. A LOD error mixes position with weighted normals and UVs and is summed over the chain, so it is not the distance a surface moved: on a Far proxy's roof slab the simplifier's measure read about a tenth under the true drop.

A chain may end early. It ends at the first LOD that keeps more than 85% of the one before, though a later step might collapse more; a Mesh of locked or faceted Primitives keeps LOD 0 alone.

No error limit. Every LOD halves whatever it costs, so a coarse LOD of a small Mesh may stray by metres, as town's ground does at 86 m for one triangle; the selection threshold, not the cook, keeps it from being drawn near.

A Thin Primitive halves nothing. A Mesh of tubes draws its LOD 0 at every distance until the culling pass drops it, once its widest tube keeps no sample.

Rounding outside the cook. Sines and cosines in town's tubes and rotations through glam's SIMD in the scenes are left as they are, because every machine's hash agrees today; a change to their inputs can move a hash on one machine alone.

Tangents only from MikkTSpace. A glTF Primitive's own tangents are ignored and recomputed, and a degenerate face gets a default tangent.

History

The Mesh cook first replaced 48-byte runtime vertices with the welded 28-byte QTangent vertex and meshoptimizer's ordering and codecs, then cut each Primitive into Meshlets for the culling pass. The far field brought the LOD chain into the Blob, with each LOD's error and its own indices and Meshlets on LOD 0's vertices; then Thin Primitives, declared at their source with their radius checked and stored, and kept whole at every LOD; and, when CI's machines were found to cook other chains than the M4 Pro, the scalar arithmetic and unfused build that make a Blob the same bytes everywhere. This page describes the mechanism as it is.
