## Shared derivation: `town` scaled to the 2 km view

`town` covers 1 km² (`src/scenes/town.rs:171`, `TOWN_HALF_SIZE` 500 m). Three figures for its Instance count are in the tree, and the rows below say which they use: the module doc's "about 250,000 Instances" (`src/scenes/mod.rs:63-65`, reader R); the profile's measured 254,256 (`docs/performance.md:1023`, reader A); and the expected value of the generator's draws, 259,175 (reader W, derived below). The verifier pinned the spawned count: `praxis export-scene town <dir>` writes 254,256 Renderables (log `scene exported scene="town" frame=59 renderables=254256`), 244,256 static and 10,000 Moving, the profile's figure; this draw built 343 blocks and 57 parks (109,760 railing posts / 80 / 4; 7,825 trees = 6,400 + 57 × 25) against 352 and 48 expected, which accounts for the 1.9%. They differ by under 4%, less than the assumptions that follow. The Baseline draws 137,730 of them at the median frame (`bench/baselines/apple-m4-pro/town.json`, `instances_drawn`), after culling.

A 2 km view radius covers a disc of π × (2 km)² = 12.566 km²; the square of 256 m Chunks that holds that disc is 4 km × 4 km = 16 km² (reader W) or, counted as the 16 × 16 Chunks whose square contains it, 16.78 km² (reader A). Rows give the disc figure first. The assumption throughout: the world beyond the Reference scene is as dense as `town` everywhere, and no HLOD merges far Instances; ADR 0013's "100k visible Instances" is the count after culling and LOD, not the Resident count.

- Instances the spawn places, as the expected value of the generator's draws (every constant from `src/scenes/town.rs:168-253`):
  - `BLOCK_HALF_SIDE` = (50 − 12) / 2 = 19 m (`:176`); `BUILDING_HALF_SIDE` = (19 − 2.5 − 0.5) / 2 = 8 m (`:182`).
  - Per building (`:438-528`): 1 wall; windows = 2 fronts × storeys × `WINDOW_COLUMNS` 4, storeys uniform over {3, 4, 5, 6} (`MIN_STOREYS` 3 + floor(r × `STOREY_RANGE` 4), `:445-446`), mean 4.5, so 36 windows; railing posts 4 sides × `RAILING_POSTS_PER_SIDE` 20 = 80, plus 4 rails; roof plant `ROOF_CELLS_PER_SIDE`² = 36 cells, 95% occupied (`:507-514`), 34.2. Total 1 + 36 + 80 + 4 + 34.2 = 155.2.

Reader R's further assumptions:

- **Chunk.** 256 m edge (ADR 0018, `docs/adr/0018-...md:26`): 65,536 m² = 0.0655 km²; at town density 250,000 × 0.0655 = 16,384 Instances and 1,024 × 0.0655 = 67 lamps per Chunk; 12.566 / 0.0655 = 192 Chunks inside the 2 km view.
- **Camera.** 45° vertical field of view at 1440p (ADR 0014, `docs/adr/0014-...md:7`): the view frustum is 2 d tan 22.5° = 0.828 d tall and (16/9) × 0.828 d = 1.473 d wide at depth d.
- **f32 rounding.** A coordinate in [2^k, 2^(k+1)) m has a step of 2^(k−23) m and rounds by half of it: in [512, 1,024) m by 0.031 mm; in [1,024, 2,048) m by 0.061 mm; in [8,192, 16,384) m by 0.49 mm. ADR 0014's bar is 0.043 mm (`docs/adr/0014-...md:7`).

Reader A's further assumptions:

- **Assumption D (density)**, defined by the verifier, as rows A02, A27, A29 and A30 use it without a definition: town's measured 254,256 Instances per km² (`docs/performance.md:1023`; `praxis export-scene town`) over the 2 km disc, 254,256 × 12.566 = 3,195,000 resident Instances, 16,663 per 256 m Chunk.
- **Assumption G (unique geometry)**: no source states the unique Meshes, Materials or Textures resident within 2 km. Where a row needs them it uses SciFiHelmet as the unit of a hero Mesh, 14,127 vertices, 70,074 indices (23,358 triangles) and 278 Meshlets as it cooks (`docs/performance.md:3059-3061`; the helmet capture logs `Mesh loaded vertices=14127 indices=70074 meshlets=278`; the glTF holds 70,074 unwelded vertices, `docs/performance.md:3045-3046`), and a resident set of 100 such Meshes, with LOD chains adding one third (a chain of quarter-sized levels sums to 4/3), as the smallest open-world library the reader finds credible; it is an assumption, not a source, and the breakpoints are given in units of helmet-class Meshes so the owner can substitute another figure. 100 helmets = 1,412,700 vertices (39.6 MB at 28 bytes); with LOD 1,883,600 vertices (52.7 MB).
- **Assumption T (bound Textures)**: `texture-wall` binds 10,005 Textures, the gate scene (`docs/performance.md:3001-3002`); measured per bound Texture per frame on the M4 Pro: 0.21 µs of CPU with no rebuild, and 0.25 to 0.30 µs per Texture per bind group build (`docs/performance.md:3018-3022`). Metal only; Vulkan and DX12 unmeasured.
- Powers of two written out: 16 MiB = 16,777,216 bytes; 128 MiB = 134,217,728; 2^31 − 1 = 2,147,483,647; 2^16 = 65,536; 2^18 = 262,144; 2^24 − 1 = 16,777,215; 2^28 = 268,435,456; 2^32 = 4,294,967,296.

## M3 choices M4 must undo

Each item: what the code does today, with its `path:line`; the clause that
makes it an M3 choice; the milestone that undoes it, and what replaces it
where an ADR or the map issue already says. A capacity that merely grows is
a row in the table above; an item is here because growing it changes the
mechanism. Readers R (render), A (assets, cook and the Capability floor)
and W (world and simulation) each listed their module's; the items below
merge them, one per mechanism.

**The world**

1. **One scene, spawned whole, before the first frame.** `Simulation::new` picks one spawn function per `ReferenceScene` and runs it once (`src/state.rs:77-93`), then propagates every World transform (`:104-108`); `State::new` cooks the scene's Assets and loads them all through `PoolLoader` on the main thread before returning (`src/state.rs:159-187`); the window shell calls `State::new` once with the one scene the command named (`src/lib.rs:175-183`, `src/bin/praxis.rs:97`). Clause: a Chunk "loads and unloads as a unit" (`CONTEXT.md`, Chunk); "Chunks inside the load radius are requested ahead" (ADR 0017:15). Undone by M4 ("256 m Chunk streaming over 10 × 10 km", the ladder), which replaces the spawn-at-start with Chunk loads and unloads over the frame.
2. **No Chunk and no Placement exist; a scene's Placements are a `Vec` built in memory at launch, and the cooker enforces no pivot range.** `placements()` returns `Vec<TownRenderable>` and `spawn_town` spawns them straight into the ECS World (`src/scenes/town.rs:343-367`, `:756-810`); every other scene but the helmet's (`src/state.rs:201`) spawns inline (`src/scenes/mod.rs:537-1037`); a search of `src/` for `Chunk` or `Placement` finds no type, only slice chunking. `src/cook/mesh.rs:104-116` computes a Mesh's bounds and refuses nothing. Clause: ADR 0018:26, "A Chunk's edge is 256 m ... A v1 Chunk is a list of Placements. Each holds a Mesh Asset ID, an f64 translation from the Chunk origin, and f32 rotation and scale"; ADR 0014:22, "cooked meshes stay within 128 m of their pivot". Undone by M4 ("cooked Chunks and Placements" moved into M4 from M2, the map issue): the cooker writes Chunks and Placements, the runtime spawns from them, and an HLOD proxy spanning a Chunk (181 m from its centre) either fails a check or takes a Chunk-local pivot; what replaces the convention is unsaid (Finding L4-06).
3. **A Mesh's runtime identity is its load order, not its Asset ID.** `MeshPool::load_for` takes no Asset ID and hands out `MeshHandle(self.meshes.len())` (`src/assets/pools.rs:149`, `:187`); `PoolLoader::mesh` passes none (`src/assets/loader.rs:56-62`), so two loads of one Asset ID are two Meshes (`crowd` relies on it, one Mesh per figure, `src/scenes/mod.rs:1019`, `:1024`), and no map from Asset ID to resident Handle exists for Meshes or Materials (the Material pool keeps one for Textures only, `src/assets/material.rs:145-146`; `MaterialPool::load` inserts on every call, `:259-290`, so `town` registers 32 Material entries for 18 distinct Materials). Scenes name Meshes in code and derive the ID at run time: `ReferenceScene::mesh_id(name)` (`src/scenes/mod.rs:113-116`) at `:541`, `:567`, `:604`, `:632`, `:709`, `:812`, `:874`, `:984-990`; `TownMesh::name` (`src/scenes/town.rs:76-98`, loaded at `:758-761`); `AssetName::Name("SciFiHelmet")` (`src/state.rs:203`) and `"NormalTangentTest_low"` (`src/scenes/mod.rs:581`). Clause: ADR 0018:3, "Asset IDs are written into Materials, Chunks, Scenes and saves"; a Placement "holds a Mesh Asset ID" (0018:26). Undone by M4: a Chunk streaming in must find the Handle of a Mesh another resident Chunk already loaded, and a Chunk streaming out must know when a Mesh's last user left, so the Mesh and Material pools need an Asset ID to Handle residency map and a use count; what replaces the per-figure copies of `crowd` is M7's (skinning).
4. **Nothing is ever evicted or unloaded: a push-only Mesh table, Arenas whose `free` nothing calls, Materials never deleted, every mip of every Texture resident.** A Handle is the table index at load (`src/assets/pools.rs:187`, `:298-302`); `Arena::free` (`src/assets/pools.rs:568-572`) is called only to roll back a refused load (`:514-518`); no `MeshPool` method frees a Mesh, its BLAS (`:339`) or its Arena ranges, and no path removes from `blases`, `refitted_blases` or `meshes`; the Arenas are "created at startup and never grown" (`CONTEXT.md`, Arena; ADR 0017:17). Clause: ADR 0017:16, "the finest mip of the lowest-priority texture goes first, least recently used breaking ties"; ADR 0013:3's split "for geometry arenas, the texture pool, Resident tails ... whose sizes M4 sets"; the ladder's M4 line, "a late mip is blur, never a hole".
