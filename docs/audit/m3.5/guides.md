# Audit lane 8, guides

The lane's report, in the form `docs/agents/audit.md` gives: the 28
volumes under `docs/guides/` and the hub read against the code at the
frozen commit `8bc34eb18fcfb79307193551e96619316aef566c`, the code
winning. Per volume, the mechanism it describes, the page in the guide's
shape (`docs/agents/workflow.md`, PRs) that absorbs it, the slice that
writes that page, and which of its text is history; per mechanism in the
frame, the volumes that cover it. Lane 8 is not a defect lane: its
Findings are the two kinds its brief names, a volume claim the code no
longer matches and a mechanism no volume describes, both `drift`. Seven
readers at high effort, each with the lane brief verbatim and one
appendix, read the volumes in full by subsystem (prepare and the two
visibility prototypes; culling, the depth pyramid and GPU ordering; the
lit pass and texture sampling; lights and HDR; acceleration structures;
shadows; timing, the bench and `town`); the session read the hub and
inverted the readers' rows into the frame table. One adversarial
verifier reopened every Finding. Finding ids carry the reader's letter
so that the rows never collide: `L8-A` to `L8-G` the readers, `L8-H` the
hub and the mechanisms no volume covers. Every `where` is a `path:line`
at the frozen commit or a volume's section. The guide slices execute
from *The slices*; triage 3 decides their final cut from the re-cut
proposals.

## Summary

Every step of the frame has volumes that describe it, and the 28 volumes' live content maps onto pages in the shape without loss: of the 27 mechanisms in the frame table only Frame stats' CPU side (the recorder, its stages and the allocation counter) and the Bench history's Experiments and release-build records have no volume, and the pages for them are written from code; four pages the slices need are named by no slice yet (`blas`, `cook-texture`, `reference-scenes` and the light count pass), and one slice, the shadow rays', overlaps the hub slice's shadow mask page almost entirely. The verifier kept 30 of 31 Findings, which fold to 25 distinct drifts; six are load-bearing (the staging slot's map request moved two writes later, the frame's draw buffer grown to 72 bytes, the lit pass's normal derivatives, the light buffer's `unshadowed_range` and its shadowed-first order, the dynamic TLAS's box skip, and the depth passes' exception to the render-pass timing rule), and nearly all are one slip: a later change to `main` that did not touch the volume's Status line, the largest being ADR 0019's reopen clause, shown as pending or live in five volumes after the ADR recorded it spent. The guide slices execute from *The slices*, each listing the pages it writes, the chapters it absorbs and the volumes it drops; the re-cut proposals are triage 3's to decide, and since each volume a Finding touches is dropped once its page lands, the Findings read best as inputs to the slices rather than as fix tickets of their own.
