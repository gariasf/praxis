at the frozen commit `8bc34eb18fcfb79307193551e96619316aef566c`; CI's

`correctness` where an uncovered seam or an oracle-less function carries

`docs/audit/deviations.md` holds no row at this commit.

## Summary

The tests hold what the tickets asked of them: of 138 Verification seams since M0, 119 are covered by a test or CI step that fails when the claim breaks, 17 claimed no test, 2 are partly covered and none is uncovered; the Golden tolerances stand at 1 of 255 and 0 pixels on hardware, the one loosening in their history (8 pixels on hardware, for one day) explained in its commit and reverted; and 34 of the 35 shading functions have an independent oracle. CI over the audited milestones shows one flake among its 161 failed or cancelled runs, a one-second bound on a frame's GPU time that a slow lavapipe runner broke twice on 2026-10-13 and never since, and no flake the milestones ticketed left its failure text in any CI log. The verifier confirmed 6 Findings, narrowed 3 and killed 1, all 9 survivors `health`; the one that weighs most is that the only required check on `main` runs `cargo test` with no adapter, where 96 GPU tests, the Goldens among them, pass by skipping, so the lavapipe Goldens gate a merge only through the workflow's rule to merge when CI is green, and the rest are gaps in what fails: the verify command run by no test, one Reference scene without a Golden, one helper without a direct oracle, and four gaps in what three conventions rules catch.

### Notes

1. Timing claims are judged by hand, not by the verify command. Every seam that says a GPU frame, gate, pass time or CPU line "must not regress" is covered by `praxis compare`'s time rules (`a_time_warns_above_5_percent_and_fails_above_10`, src/bench/mod.rs:1044; `the_gate_fails_over_6_ms_and_both_lines_against_their_baselines`, src/bench/mod.rs:1145; `the_dynamic_tlas_fails_over_its_ceilings`, src/bench/mod.rs:1168), but `scripts/verify.sh` runs `praxis bench --counters` and `praxis compare --ci` (scripts/verify.sh:52 and :53), and `--ci` ignores times (`ci_mode_ignores_times_and_fails_on_counters`, src/bench/mod.rs:1089); CI's `software-bench` job does the same on lavapipe (.github/workflows/ci.yml:178 and :181). A time regression is caught only by a hand run of `praxis compare` without `--ci`, or by the Bench history's Suspects in the nightly sweep. This is the bench's design (times are noisy, counters repeat), so the rows stay `covered`; the lane on CI history or the performance lane may weigh whether that gap is acceptable. It applies to the rows of the shadow terminator, the specular antialiasing, the critical path, the edge fragments, the shadow edge rays, the grazing shadow rays, the shared-edge normals, the TLAS binding, the spin-wait, the queued frames, the two-mode pass times, the shadow ray cost, the CPU frame offender, the depth pyramid offender, the dynamic TLAS skip, the no-shadow point lights and the shadow mask occupancy.
