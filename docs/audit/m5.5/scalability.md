M6's scope only: probe GI, Interior volumes and reflection cubemaps, their rays

## Summary

Of the 50 fixed capacities, binding floors and buffer sizes that M6's scope
touches, 20 survive every option the GI survey leaves M6's charting, 26
survive or must change by the option taken, and 4 must change under every
option: the scene and pass buffers' Budget, 98.19% held by fixed capacities on
all 47 Baselines with 4,251,548 bytes free, where the prototype's 8,192 probes
alone need 9,568,256 to 10,485,760 bytes of state at published encodings (M01,
M02); the index Arena, bound to no stage, under a Mesh table that names no
index range, which every option that shades a hit needs (B17); and a freed
Instance slot taken again the next frame while the static TLAS binds its
change 7 frames later on the M4 Pro and 15 to 16 on the RTX 2070 SUPER, so a
hit read through its slot can read another Instance (B19). Of 98 clause rows
over ADRs 0001 to 0027, 72 survive, 21 split by option and 5 must change under
every option (the seventh Budget as ADRs 0013 and 0017 size it, ADR 0020's
constant ambient term, the Reference scene ADR 0020's criteria are read on,
which neither `town` nor `world` provides with no interior, door or moving
sun, and ADR 0023's hand-set EV100), and eleven M4 and M5 choices M6 must undo
are listed, among them the Channel textures bound for the fragment stage
alone, a Far proxy's entry change waiting 120 frames for a build, the TLAS
mask's eight bits all taken, and the birds that fill the dynamic TLAS to its
10,000 on both dense scenes. On the M4 Pro 2 ms of the probe-ray pass alone
buys 1.33 to 1.80 million probe rays a frame, ray length changing nothing on
`world`, while `town`'s GPU frame already breaches its 12 ms Budget, so any
probe pass widens a breach that stands; the one Finding is a drift in the GI
survey's account of what a hit can read, placed in fog until M6's charting.
No code must change before M6 can start, so the lane files no fix; the owner
confirmed every verdict and all eighteen choices in the
[decision comment](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/17#issuecomment-7109367684),
and nothing here charts M6.

**The probe-ray prototype and the rays it measured**

- **P, the probe-ray prototype** (all readers). A 32 × 8 × 32 grid of probes 4 m apart, 8,192 probes, 128 × 32 × 128 m, tracing 8, 64 or 256 rays a probe: 65,536, 524,288 or 2,097,152 rays a frame, each to its closest hit in both TLASes, with a triangle fetch and one sun ray from each hit; it shades nothing (`prototype-ticket.md:11`, `:17-18`, `:36`; `prototype-resolution.md:13-24`, `:78`, `:82-83`). The camera is held still (`:82`). 2 ms of the pass alone buys 1,362,000 rays on `world`'s densest view, 1,802,000 on `town` with the dynamic TLAS empty and 1,331,000 with 10,000 Moving Instances (`:38-43`). On `town`, 2,097,152 rays make 1,277,424 hits (`:60-62`). Its pass binds group 2 as the shadow mask pass does and a group 3 of its own: two uniforms and six storage buffers (prototype `src/render/bind_groups.rs:282-303`, `src/render/probe_ray_pass.rs:154-160`). M4 Pro only: the RTX 2070 SUPER's leg is not in, and every row resting on P says so.
- **P-entries.** The prototype's `world` camera holds 253,548 static TLAS entries, the most of any camera on the 64 m grid (`prototype-resolution.md:82`).

**Published probe forms (survey `docs/research/global-illumination-and-lighting.md`, its Refresh section)**
