# Textures stream by mip, geometry lives in fixed arenas

Textures are ordinary mip chains streamed whole mip at a time in wgpu's safe API, chosen by CPU heuristics; vertices and indices live in fixed arenas sized from the Budget at device creation and sub-allocated by an offset allocator. This is option A of `docs/research/gpu-memory-residency-streaming.md`: the only option that keeps hardware trilinear and anisotropic filtering unchanged and adds no new artefact class (a late mip shows as blur, never as a crack or a capped anisotropy), and it needs only Tier 1 bindless. Accepted 2026-10-12 ([#21](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/21)). Partly implemented: each Channel is a partially bound binding array of texture arrays, one per Texture class of Textures sized to their source (#101, #622), loaded from cooked Blobs through a `StagingBelt` and sampled trilinear, the colour Channels at 16x anisotropy and the data Channels at 4x, a choice of cost and image the samplers make, not a cap the streaming design imposes (amended 2026-10-22, [#184](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/184)), a Texture a Chunk loads arriving as its Resident tail, its finer mips streaming in by the priority below through the IO pool, while one a spawn loads keeps its full mip chain (amended 2026-11-08, [#666](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/666)), with the eviction below making room for a move into a full Channel (amended 2026-11-08, [#667](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/667)), and geometry loads through the same belt into one vertex and one index arena, a provisional 16 MiB each until #625 sized them from the Budget, which refuse a Mesh that does not fit (#145), and into three Meshlet arenas, of the same size until then, for the Meshlets, their vertices and their triangles (amended 2026-10-20, [#194](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/194)). `bench/budgets.json` holds a Budget per kind, which ADR 0013 records, and at device creation the pools derive their capacities from them, each Arena and table at its share of its kind's Budget below, and each Channel a Texture class per 64 KiB of the texture pool's fifth, up to what the Capability floor binds (amended 2026-11-06, [#625](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/625); shares since 2026-11-09, [#671](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/671)).

## Considered options

- Software virtual texturing: a fixed page cache, but the tile border caps anisotropy (a 4-texel border allows 8x), mips smaller than a tile are missing, and pages arrive a frame or more late.
- Hardware sparse textures through wgpu-hal: per-backend native code that wgpu-core does not track, never tried, and Intel's sample shows black cracks where anisotropic sampling reaches unmapped tiles. It stays the fallback if the bind-group cost below fails its gate.
- Virtual geometry pages: they depend on the rasterization path, so they belong to [Visibility strategy for the main pass](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/22); ADR 0019 turned virtual geometry down until its reopen condition holds, and if it reopens, the vertex arena becomes a page pool there.
- Growable geometry buffers, by CPU re-upload (as before #145) or GPU copy: growth briefly holds the old and new buffers, and the CPU re-upload keeps a shadow of every vertex.

## Consequences

- Each Channel is one partially bound binding array (`TEXTURE_BINDING_ARRAY`, `PARTIALLY_BOUND_BINDING_ARRAY`) of texture arrays in one bind group, an array per Texture class: the Textures of one shape, size, mip count and format alike, none padded or resampled to share one. A Layer names a class and a slice of its array, Layer 0 stays the neutral default, and the fixed 8 Layers per Channel go. A class's slice count is fixed when it opens, at most the Capability floor's 256 array layers (ADR 0021) [ref:wgpu-types-30-max-texture-array-layers-default], and a full class's shape opens another; an empty slice still holds memory, which `gpu_memory_bytes` counts. A Texture whose resident mips change moves to the class of its new shape by copy. Metal's `useResource`, which wgpu-hal calls per bound view [ref:wgpu-hal-30-metal-resources-to-use-by-view], and wgpu-core's tracking then follow the classes, not the Textures: per Texture, 10,005 `useResource` calls cost 12.8 to 13.5 ms of kernel scheduling in a probe outside wgpu, against texture-wall's 13.8 to 14.3 ms gate, besides wgpu-core's tracking (`docs/research/bindless-residency-cost.md`, option B; amended 2026-11-06, [#622](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/622)). Residency changes are batched so that bind group is rebuilt at most once per frame. Gate, on [#153](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/153) (moved from #101, which had no 10k Textures to measure): the rebuild plus Metal's per-texture `useResource` on each `set_bind_group` [ref:wgpu-hal-30-metal-use-resource], measured at 10k textures on the M4 Pro, fits ADR 0013's 6 ms critical path; if not, batch over several frames, then hardware sparse. Measured in #153 at the line (`docs/performance.md`) and recorded as marginal; a survey before M4's residency work was to decide what follows ([#173](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/pull/173)), and M4's charting chose that survey's option B, above ([map decision 3](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/609), #599, #622).
- Priority is computed on the CPU: the finest mip any visible Instance needs, from distance and the Primitive's UV texel density (stored at cook time, per Primitive because each Primitive's Material has its own Textures; amended 2026-10-12, ADR 0018) against the 1440p pixel footprint; Chunks inside the load radius are requested ahead. No GPU feedback readback until a measured miss rate asks for it. The visible Instances are the drawn Placements of the loaded Chunks, frustum aside, so turning the camera never makes a mip late; each is judged at the nearest point of the sphere around its Mesh's bounds, at its smallest scale, against the footprint of a pixel at the corner of a 16:9 view 1,440 pixels tall, where a pixel's angle is narrowest; and a Texture's mips from the finest needed to its finest Resident are requested in one read, finest-needed first (amended 2026-11-08, [#666](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/666)). The Materials rays reach off screen, those of the static TLAS's Instances, take the Resident tail as their floor, which the next consequence keeps loaded: shadow rays are opaque and sample no Texture, and a ray needs texels no finer than its share of the sphere is wide at its hit, √(4π/N) metres per metre for N rays a probe, so the tails of the Textures the Reference scenes stream, whose texels span 10.4 to 15.6 mm of surface, serve a probe ray from 7 cm of its probe on at 256 rays a probe and from 14 cm at 1,024. A finer floor waits for a ray that samples a Texture nearer its origin than that: an alpha-tested shadow ray, or a ray count M6 chooses (amended 2026-11-08, [#668](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/668)).
- Each texture's Resident tail (mips of 64 KiB and smaller) never leaves. When finer mips arrive, the Texture moves to the class of its mips from its finest Resident one and its tail's slice stays taken, charged twice in `resident_tails` (87,408 bytes for a 2,048² BC7 or BC5 Texture, 1.6% of its chain), so eviction falls back to it without a copy (amended 2026-11-08, [#666](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/666)). When a request would exceed the texture pool's capacity, which is set from its Budget, the finest mip of the lowest-priority texture goes first, least recently used breaking ties; a mip loads at priority p and is evicted only below p − 1, so it does not thrash. As a Channel's capacity is its Texture classes and a class keeps its slices once it opens, the candidates are the Textures whose chain lies in the very class the request needs, whose slice it takes; the one evicted moves to the class of its mips from the next, or falls back to its tail only when it needs no finer mip than the tail's, so it never re-requests what it lost; and a request is made only when its move finds room, free or by an eviction (amended 2026-11-08, [#667](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/667)).
- One vertex arena and one index arena, and the Meshlet arenas, each at most 2^31 − 1 bytes (wgpu-hal 30's `max_buffer_size` on DX12, whose API exposes no maximum, and on Linux and Android Vulkan other than NVIDIA's [ref:wgpu-hal-30-dx12-max-buffer] [ref:wgpu-hal-30-vulkan-max-buffer]; amended 2026-10-26), sub-allocated with the `offset-allocator` crate (a port of Aaltonen's OffsetAllocator, a TLSF-like allocator, MIT [ref:offset-allocator-tlsf] [ref:offset-allocator-0.2.0-port]; new dependency, justified in the implementing PR). Since #193 the vertex shader reads the whole vertex arena through one storage binding (ADR 0019), so the Capability floor's 128 MiB `max_storage_buffer_binding_size` caps that arena until its binding splits or the floor rises (ADR 0021). No growth at runtime: a load that does not fit is refused and logged. The CPU shadow of vertex and index data goes.
- The 8 GB of ADR 0013 splits into Budgets for geometry arenas, the texture pool, Resident tails, render targets, acceleration structures and staging; M4 sets their sizes ([decision](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/17#issuecomment-6169534752)), which ADR 0013 records. Each capacity is its share of its kind's Budget at its real bytes per entry: the vertex, index, Meshlet, Meshlet vertex and Meshlet triangle Arenas take 48.7%, 31.7%, 0.6%, 8.5% and 10.5% of the geometry Budget, as `helmet`'s, `town`'s and the world scene's Meshes fill them, and the Mesh table an entry per 4,488 bytes of it, `town`'s mean Mesh; the Material table an entry per 19,267,584 bytes of the texture pool's, the largest Material of the Reference scenes, `helmet`'s, past its Resident tails; the static TLAS's two TLASes 57% of the acceleration structures', at 289 bytes an entry, the most one costs on the adapters benched, its driver's bytes and wgpu-core's Instance record; the Instance slots 30% of the scene and pass buffers', at 136.2 bytes a slot with its offsets and visibility bit, in whole scan blocks, and the lights 0.1%, at 48 bytes; the scene and pass buffers' Budget also holds the two tables and the passes' buffers of fixed size, and the staging Budget the frame staging's slots at their largest. The split leaves the vertex Arena at 40,852,520 bytes, under the floor's 128 MiB binding, so its binding stays whole (amended 2026-11-09, [#671](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/671)). A seventh kind holds what none of those six does: the scene and pass buffers, the Instance and light buffers, the Mesh and Material tables and the passes' own buffers. The Renderer and its Pools create the buffers, textures and TLASes they count through one ledger, and charge it their BLASes' bytes; it keeps a running total per kind from creation until drop, and `gpu_memory_bytes` is the sum of those totals. What ADR 0013 leaves out, the GPU stats readback and the load staging, bypasses it (amended 2026-11-05, [decision](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/624#issuecomment-6666427496)). A `TRANSIENT_ATTACHMENT` counts as zero only when `transient_saves_memory` is `Some(true)` [ref:wgpu-types-30-transient-saves-memory]. Acceleration structures join the ledger (amended 2026-10-20, [#202](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/202)), each BLAS and TLAS at the size its driver gives through wgpu-hal's build-sizes query, asked as wgpu-core asks it at creation [ref:wgpu-30-blas-size-at-create] [ref:wgpu-30-tlas-size-at-create], and each TLAS with wgpu-core's buffer of its Instances, 72 bytes a slot on Metal and 64 elsewhere [ref:wgpu-30-tlas-instance-buffer] [ref:wgpu-hal-30-metal-instance-bytes] [ref:wgpu-hal-30-instance-bytes]. A compacted BLAS's size stays an estimate, since wgpu-core keeps the size it reads crate-private [ref:wgpu-30-blas-compacted-size-private] and reading it again takes a query recorded through wgpu-hal outside wgpu's tracking, and another wait: the fraction of its built size compaction kept where measured, 45.5 of 94 bytes per triangle on the M4 Pro, 29.2 of 70.7 on the RTX 2070 SUPER (Vulkan, NVIDIA 621.01) and all of it on lavapipe (Mesa 25.2.8), picked by the adapter's vendor, then its backend: NVIDIA's on an NVIDIA GPU on any backend, lavapipe's on any other Vulkan adapter, the M4 Pro's otherwise (amended 2026-11-04, [#543](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/543); exact sizes amended 2026-11-06, [#664](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/664)).
- Streaming uploads go through a `StagingBelt`, never `Queue::write_texture`, which allocates fresh staging on every call [ref:wgpu-core-30-write-texture-staging]. The belt's `chunk_size` is only its allocation unit (it allocates more on demand) [ref:wgpu-30-staging-belt-chunk-size], so the engine caps the bytes written into it per frame at the staging Budget, and an upload past the cap waits for a later frame; the IO pool (ADR 0016) decodes into CPU memory and the main thread copies it into the belt. The cap holds each frame's streamed Chunk loads to a third of the staging Budget, as the frames in flight and the one being written can each hold a belt, and, since the frame staging's slots take their share first, a third of what those slots at their largest leave of it (amended 2026-11-09, [#671](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/671)); each Chunk is sized from its decoded Assets before it loads, nearest the camera first, and one that alone writes past the cap loads with no other writes in its frame rather than never. A capture's Settled wait loads past the cap, so its load order stays fixed, and a spawn's loads are not capped (amended 2026-11-08, [#660](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/660)). A frame's Chunk loads also place at most 131,072 Instances, since prepare and the static TLAS take each on the main thread in the frame its Chunk loads, whatever bytes it writes; a Chunk that alone places more loads alone, and a Texture read on the main thread for a Chunk, one a load still in flight claimed, is its Resident tail alone, as the IO pool reads it (amended 2026-11-09, [#743](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/743)); a Chunk that needs an Asset a Chunk read in flight claimed waits for the reads in flight when its own came back instead, so the main thread reads one only after a failed read or a refused load left it claimed (amended 2026-11-12, [#773](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/773)).
- The cooked format (ADR 0011) stores each mip as a separately loadable byte range of the Texture's Blob, with the Resident tail as one range, and the Mesh Blob each Primitive's UV texel density (ADR 0018; amended 2026-11-04, [#543](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/543); stored since 2026-11-06, [#616](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/616)).
