# Ray-traced probe GI and hard ray-traced shadows

Indirect diffuse light comes from ray-traced irradiance probes in camera-centred clipmap cascades (DDGI form: irradiance plus depth moments per probe, a Chebyshev visibility test against leaks), updated a subset per frame and shaded from position and normal in ADR 0019's lit pass. Direct light is one hard shadow ray per pixel per light, and, where a directional light's verdict differs from a neighbour's, or where the surface's nearest sample turns from it and another of its samples faces it, one from each other sample that shows the same surface (amended 2026-10-30, [#358](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/358), from `docs/research/shadow-edge-antialiasing.md`'s option B: sun shadow edges beside 4x MSAA geometry stepped by whole pixels; on `town` the extra rays cost the M4 Pro's GPU frame 2.7 to 3.1 ms, against a 4 ms ceiling the owner set; amended 2026-10-31, [#407](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/407): at a silhouette the nearest triangle is seen nearly edge-on and the surface behind it lies within a pixel's width of its plane, so the pixel shows one surface by depth, and the nearest sample's turn hid the light from every sample; 11 pixels of `crowd`'s frame 59 at 2560×1440 gave the lit ground or figure behind a silhouette a figure's sun verdict; such pixels now join the edge rays, as do those along a smooth surface's terminator, which now resolves per sample, and the edge rays list 13.8% of `town`'s pixels at frame 1200 rather than 12.4%, cost its GPU frame on the M4 Pro 0.57 to 0.60 ms, and halve the error of the pixels they change against a render at three times the size, box-filtered). This is option C of `docs/research/global-illumination-and-lighting.md`, and it supersedes ADR 0008's lighting clause. Direct light is Temporal class (a); indirect light is class (b), and class (c) is used nowhere. The sun and sky change continuously (time of day, weather), every light bounces within the probe cascades, and moving geometry changes indirect light, because the probe rays read the same TLAS as the shadow rays (amended 2026-11-27, [decision](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/17#issuecomment-7223047291), at M6's charting: a probe ray's hit is shaded with its Instance's Bounce colour, the mean base colour and emissive of its Mesh's Materials or of the Material its Placement overrides them with, and lit by the sun through one shadow ray, by the sky where the ray misses, which is the scene's ambient illuminance over π from every direction until an atmosphere replaces it, by each point light a world-space Light grid lists at the hit, through a shadow ray of its own and up to a cap that Frame stats count, and by the Probes as the frame before left them, which carry the further bounces; a light can be kept out of the Probe update, and past the Load radius, which the Light grid does not cover and where no Chunk's point light is in the frame, only the sun, the sky and emissive surfaces bounce. No cache of a Probe's hits is kept to relight without a trace: with the dynamic TLAS at its 10,000 Moving Instances, 2 ms buys 1,331,000 probe rays on `town` on the M4 Pro (`docs/audit/m5.5/scalability.md`) and 3,351,000 on the RTX 2070 SUPER, the second extended past its measured rows ([lane 4](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/17#issuecomment-7109367684)), each ray with the sun ray from its hit and no Material or light work, the pass timed alone at one engine source, against the rays a frame of the published probe updates that state a count, from about 66,000 where most probes are relit from a cache [ref:leblanc-conte-2025-acs-probe-timings] to 524,288 where every probe is traced [ref:majercik-2019-ddgi-timings] (`docs/research/global-illumination-and-lighting.md`, Refresh)). Accepted 2026-10-13 ([#65](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/65)). Partly implemented: each Mesh rays trace gets a BLAS at load, compacted unless it is loaded to be refitted or rebuilt each frame, as the crowd's refit and rebuild forms load theirs (amended 2026-11-04, [#543](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/543)), and the static TLAS covered the static Instances in a frame anchored at load, rebuilt in the frame its static Instances changed, as when one is spawned or despawned, until it was re-anchored and rebuilt off the frame's critical path on 2026-11-08, below ([#202](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/202)); it holds exactly the static Instances of the loaded, drawn Chunks, the drawn Far proxies (since 2026-11-18, [#816](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/816)) and the static spawned Renderables, each in an entry of its own whatever its slot, and leaves out only those written while it is full, which take the entries it frees (amended 2026-11-07, [#658](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/658): its entries had been the Instance slots below its capacity, and Chunks take their ranges of slots past the highest slot taken, so a Chunk's static Instances could fall past the capacity with entries to spare). One hard shadow ray per pixel is traced toward each directional light against the static TLAS, skipped where the light adds nothing (since #260, a light the surface's interpolated normal turns from is hidden without one; amended 2026-11-04, [#543](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/543): the test reads the interpolated normal ADR 0019's surface normals carry, not the face's), from an origin offset along the surface's normal and composed in the TLAS's frame ([#203](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/203)), and one toward each point light that reaches the pixel, ending at the light ([#205](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/205)). A compute pass traces them from the depth target, at each pixel's nearest sample and, where its other samples show another surface, at the farthest of those too, each fragment then reading its own surface's ([#357](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/357)), and, at a pixel whose verdict for one of the first seven directional lights differs from a 4-neighbour's among the lights both traced, or whose nearest sample turns from that light while another sample of its nearest surface faces it, toward that light again from each other sample that shows its nearest surface, the fragment shading it by the share of its samples that see it ([#358](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/358), [#407](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/407)); where the nearest sample turns from a point light while another sample of its nearest surface faces it, the surface's one bit for that light is the verdict of a ray from the first such sample ([#417](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/417)), into a shadow mask the lit pass reads, which holds 59 lights per pixel: a light past them casts no shadow there, and Frame stats count it ([#260](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/260), [decision](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/17#issuecomment-6369952061)). Moving Instances form the dynamic TLAS, rebuilt each frame in the static TLAS's frame from at most 10,000 of them, those in the lowest slots, and a shadow ray the static TLAS lets through is traced against it; its build's recording and submit, and the build itself on the GPU, are timed in each frame that builds it ([#204](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/204)). The `crowd` Reference scene's figures, which the GPU bends each frame, have no BLAS and cast their shadows through rigid per-bone shadow proxies, Moving Instances in that TLAS, the default form, which [#208](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/208) measures and M8 builds on ([decision](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/17#issuecomment-6410351140)). The crowd's refit Render switch instead gives each figure a BLAS, refitted to its bent shape each frame through wgpu-hal on Metal, its Instance a Moving Instance, and the dynamic TLAS's build waits for the refits on the GPU through a Metal shared event; the refits are timed on the GPU from the end of the bend pass to a marker after them ([#209](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/209)). Its rebuild form, the oracle the refits must match, rebuilds each figure's BLAS through wgpu each frame instead, and times neither those rebuilds nor the dynamic TLAS's build, which reads as zero, since a BLAS rebuilt through wgpu would fail the timing marker's query, as `DynamicTlas::mark` records (amended 2026-11-04, [#543](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/543)). A constant ambient term stands in for indirect light. Behind the `probes` Render switch, off by default, one Cascade of 32 × 16 × 32 Probes 1 m apart stands where the camera of the first frame stood, and each frame a compute pass traces 128 fixed directions from each Probe of two of its 16 levels, 262,144 rays, which read the sky where they miss and black where they hit ([#1110](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/1110)).

## Considered options

- A, ADR 0008 as written (baked lightmaps and probes, cascaded shadow maps, ray-traced GI for dynamic objects): time of day only through keyframe bakes (AC Shadows: 33 keyframes, 10 GB for 256 km²), moving lights do not bounce on static geometry, and correct lighting needs a 4 to 10 h bake per level (DOOM Eternal's figure).
- B, relightable precomputed GI: sun and sky relight, but precomputed visibility ignores moving geometry (an opened door leaves the room dark), local lights bounce only through transfer data at 9× irradiance or more, and it needs a per-Chunk bake.
- D, per-pixel ReSTIR path tracing: Temporal class (c), excluded by ADR 0008.
- E, radiance cascades: class (a) for one bounce, but in 3D shown in research scenes and described for one mobile game in a talk's abstract, which gives no figure [ref:ruan-2026-love-and-deepspace-gi] (amended 2026-11-27: this read "shown only in research scenes"), memory unpublished, 8.6 to 11.5 ms on a laptop RTX 3080. Reopens when a 3D implementation fits 3 ms on the M4 Pro on a Reference scene.
- Within C, surfels (GIBS) spawn from a G-buffer that ADR 0019 does not write; a world-space hash cache (id Tech 8) is sparser and finer but its shipped form adds class (c) passes for detail.
- Within C, what a probe ray's hit is shaded with: the full Material would bind the Material table and each Channel for the compute stage, 65,535 of the Capability floor's 65,536 binding-array elements there (`docs/audit/m5.5/scalability.md`), and stays possible behind the hit's one shading function; a G-buffer cached per probe and relit without a trace, as Assassin's Creed Shadows keeps (20 × 20 texels a probe, 55 MB for 10,240 probes [ref:leblanc-conte-2025-acs-probe-timings] [ref:leblanc-conte-2025-acs-probe-cascades]), saves rays the engine is not short of, by the figures above, and adds fixed ray directions and an invalidation at every change of geometry (2026-11-27).
- Soft sun shadows: jittered rays without a denoiser need about 4 rays per pixel, about 7 ms on the M4 Pro's open scene (one ray measured 1.8 to 2.0 ms); a denoiser is class (c). The sun's 0.53° [ref:nasa-sun-apparent-diameter] gives about 0.9 cm of penumbra per metre of occluder distance, so contact shadows stay sharp either way.

## Consequences

- Ray queries are a required capability, not a high-preset path; cascaded shadow maps are dropped.
- Indirect light converges to 90% of its value within 0.5 s of a step change (a light toggled, a door opened) and does not flicker at steady state, measured on a Reference scene; the probe update takes at most 2 ms of GPU on the dense Reference scene, judged on the M4 Pro, the reference machine, and measured on the RTX 2070 SUPER from the same engine source beside it. Both are provisional until the probes are built and measured. If the update cannot meet both, this decision reopens, once the 2 ms figure is in on both adapters, as ADR 0013 asks of a reopen trigger (amended 2026-11-14, [decision](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/17#issuecomment-6878038353): the clause named no adapter). At M6's charting (amended 2026-11-27, [decision](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/17#issuecomment-7223047291)): the 0.5 s is read on the `house` Reference scene, on the finest Cascade, for a step of the sun, a point light toggled and a door, a Moving Instance, opened; a static change counts from the frame its static TLAS build is bound, and the frames it waited stay Frame stats' own line; the coarser Cascades report their figure under no criterion. Steady state is a scene and a camera that do not change, with the Probes converged: over 64 consecutive frames no pixel's 8-bit value leaves one step of its median. A Settled frame's Probes are converged, its hold lasting until every Cascade has had a fixed count of updates, and a Fast travel resets the Probes and holds until they are filled. The 2 ms is what the Probe update adds to `town`'s GPU frame, the Probes updating against the update frozen, at a fixed count of rays a frame and with the dynamic TLAS at its 10,000 Moving Instances; the lit pass's lookup is outside it, on a line of its own beside ADR 0019's gate, its figure set from its first measurement. Hysteresis is fixed per Cascade, and thresholds that lower it on a change [ref:majercik-2021-ddgi-hysteresis-thresholds] enter only if a fixed one cannot meet the 0.5 s and steady state together.
- No bake is needed for correct lighting, and no lighting term is cooked (amended 2026-11-27, [decision](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/17#issuecomment-7223047291): this clause kept one, a per-Chunk sky-visibility field with bounced sky visibility for what is drawn beyond the probe cascades, relit by the current sky; it is struck: it relights from the sky alone, with no light from a local light and none bounced from the sun unless sky visibility stands in for the sun's shadowing, an assumption its originator lists as often wrong [ref:patry-2021-got-sky-visibility], and it would add a version of the Chunk Blob and a re-cook). What is drawn beyond the Load radius is lit by the same Probes: outer Cascades whose rays meet the Far proxies in the static TLAS, where a probe ray to 2 km costs what one to 256 m does on `world`, within 0.5% on the M4 Pro (`docs/audit/m5.5/scalability.md`) and within its rounds' spread on the RTX 2070 SUPER ([lane 4](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/17#issuecomment-7109367684)). The Cascades cover the Load radius at the least; each further one is added once priced, and past the last a surface takes sky light alone, the scene's ambient illuminance with nothing hiding it.
- Interiors are entered without a load. Walls thinner than the probe spacing leak past the Chebyshev test, so authored Interior volumes, each a set of oriented boxes under one identity, give every Probe and every shaded point a belonging, one volume or none, and a point reads only the Probes of its own (amended 2026-11-27, [decision](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/17#issuecomment-7223047291): this clause read "mask probes to their inside or outside, as Ghost of Tsushima did", whose mask is a value per surface, over tetrahedral probe sets made per streamed location [ref:patry-2021-got-interior-mask]; shapes that class each probe, and each point the probes are read at, as inside or outside are Assassin's Creed Shadows' and Ghost of Yōtei's [ref:leblanc-conte-2025-acs-interior-classification] [ref:wohllaib-2026-yotei-probes-and-interiors], and their two states leave leaks between adjacent interiors, which an identity per volume closes with the same comparison). The belonging limits which Probes a point reads, not what a probe ray sees: light through an open door arrives by rays, and the seam at an opening is blended over a band. No rule sets a wall's thickness: a thin wall under no volume leaks, a limit the `house` Reference scene shows. Visibility traced at shading time, a ray toward each of the 8 Probes a pixel reads, is 29.5 million rays at 2560×1440, about 44 ms on the M4 Pro and 18 ms on the RTX 2070 SUPER at the price of a probe ray above (computed), and is not built. A separate probe set per interior waits until a Reference scene shows the belongings failing.
- The TLAS holds the near field plus merged far proxies (ADR 0026's Far proxies), so a 100k-Instance rebuild (6 to 8 ms of CPU on the M4 Pro, wgpu-core has no refit [ref:wgpu-30-tlas-rebuild-only]) stays out of the frame; its radius is decided with Chunk streaming (M4) (amended 2026-10-19, [decision](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/17#issuecomment-6273209752)). A static build keeps the static TLAS's Anchor until the camera's is more than a Chunk's edge from it along an axis, where a build runs though no static Instance changed and re-anchors it at the camera's, so the camera adds less than 4.9 mm to a shadow ray's offset once that build is bound, and the frames drawn in between do not move the frame rays trace in (amended 2026-11-16, [#888](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/888), [decision](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/888#issuecomment-6922362307): R2's build without R1's move at every static build); the dynamic TLAS, rebuilt each frame in the bound static TLAS's frame, follows in the frame a new one is bound (amended 2026-11-08, [#663](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/663), [decision](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/609#issuecomment-6666443950), from `docs/research/acceleration-structures-for-streamed-worlds.md`'s options R1 and R2). The static rebuild is off the frame's critical path: two static TLASes, each with its own bind group, and a worker thread takes the static Instance changes the spare lacks, records and finishes its build, which the main thread submits (ADR 0016), and the spare is bound from the frame after that submit; a build lands a frame or more after its change, and a capture waits for the worker so its images never depend on the worker's timing (amended 2026-11-08, [#662](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/662), from `docs/research/acceleration-structures-for-streamed-worlds.md`'s options B and S1: on wgpu 30.0.1 a worker takes 13 to 14 ms of a 254,256-Instance rebuild off the main thread, which keeps the submit, 4.3 to 4.5 ms alone and 5.6 to 7.1 ms beside a frame's, at the cost of a second TLAS, 75.6 MB at 262,144 entries). That submit, and each frame's that binds a static TLAS, walks one dependency per distinct BLAS since `vendor/wgpu-core` carries gfx-rs/wgpu#9835 [ref:wgpu-pr-9835]: over `world`'s bench segment, wgpu-core's `Queue::submit` fell from 2.5 to 0.05 ms at the median for a build and from 1.9 to 0.05 ms for the frame, where wgpu-core 30.0.1 walked one per Instance (amended 2026-11-09, [#744](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/744), `docs/performance.md`). The near field splits in two: a static TLAS in an anchor-relative frame (ADR 0014), rebuilt only when its static Instances change (a Chunk streams in or out, a Far proxy swaps with its Chunk, or a static Renderable spawns or despawns; a Far proxy's entry change alone, as when it crosses the View distance, joins the next build and starts one only once it is 120 frames old, since every build is of every entry: on the M4 Pro `world`'s bench segment ran 402 builds with each such change starting one, against 59 with Far proxies untraced, amended 2026-11-18, [#816](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/816)) or the anchor moves, and a dynamic TLAS of Moving Instances rebuilt each frame and capped at 10k Instances (0.64 to 0.92 ms of CPU on the M4 Pro, recording plus submit, measured). Shadow and probe rays query both. A probe ray's hit reads its Bounce colour and its triangle through the Instance slot its TLAS entry names, while a freed slot is taken again the next frame and the bound static TLAS is frames older; how a hit's key outlives that is decided before hit shading is built (2026-11-27, [#1075](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/1075)). Every BLAS is built from its Mesh's LOD 0, a refitted Mesh's included, whichever LOD the main pass draws, so a LOD switch changes no BLAS and starts no static TLAS build; the index Arena, which only BLAS builds and refits read, holds LOD 0's indices alone (amended 2026-11-14, [#807](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/807), [decision](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/798#issuecomment-6875169024)).
- Rough specular reads the irradiance probes. Glossy specular's form is undecided (amended 2026-11-27, [decision](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/17#issuecomment-7223047291): this clause had it read reflection cubemaps captured at runtime by ray tracing, amortised and relit with the sun, class b, a form for which no shipped precedent was found; between those and radiance kept per probe the choice is taken when world content is charted, beside water's mirror reflections, and reads the shading of a hit that M6 builds). Mirror reflections (one ray, class a) return with water.
- Probes are smooth, so contact darkening is missing beyond what a Material's occlusion Channel authors (ADR 0018); a class (a) contact term is added when a Golden shows floating objects. The trigger is first read at the `house` Reference scene's first probe-lit captures (2026-11-27, [decision](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/17#issuecomment-7223047291)): each shipped ray-traced probe system whose deck says how a pixel is shaded puts a per-pixel pass above its probes, Temporal class (c) in each (`docs/research/global-illumination-and-lighting.md`, Refresh).
- Sun penumbrae reopen when area lights arrive or a Golden shows hard sun edges at distance.
- Later work reads these seams, and M6 builds nothing for them (2026-11-27, [decision](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/17#issuecomment-7223047291)): the lit pass holds no ray query and takes shadows and indirect light from textures, which a later quality tier keeps; fog can read a Probe's average, and Interior volumes mask media as they mask surfaces; tracing the Ray scene is one shared WGSL part, `first_intersection`, through which the shadow mask pass and the Probe update trace (2026-11-28, [#1110](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/1110): a probe ray asks it for the nearest hit, a shadow ray for the nearest and then any), and a marched terrain's traversal would join it there; water and glossy reflections read the shading of a hit. A probe ray sees what a shadow ray sees, every triangle opaque; what it sees of a canopy is decided with alpha-tested shadow rays.
