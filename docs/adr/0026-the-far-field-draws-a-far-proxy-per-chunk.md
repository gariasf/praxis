# The far field draws a Far proxy per Chunk

Between the Load radius and the View distance each Chunk draws one Far proxy in place of its Placements: a Mesh the cooker merges from the Chunk's static Placements, with a Material baked for it and, on a second Primitive, the Chunk's ground tiles under the ground's own Material. A Chunk swaps against its Far proxy at the Load radius, whole and hard; past the View distance only flat ground is drawn. Accepted 2026-11-14 ([decision](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/795#issuecomment-6874889765), items 1, 3, 8, 9 and 11; its bake as the [bake decision](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/804#issuecomment-6878696975) amends items 3 and 5, on the survey `docs/research/far-proxy-baking.md`). Implemented: the cook of a Far proxy's Mesh (#812), which also settled what the decisions left to the build: the LOD chain's cut, the pivot, and the three cases under *Settled by the cook*; and its atlas (#867), the transfer into it (#868) and the Blobs of its baked Material (#813), with what *Settled by the bake* lists; and its draw, the swap and the ground past the View distance (#815), with what *Settled by the draw* lists. Not yet: its shadows.

## Decision

- **One Far proxy per Chunk.** Every Chunk outside the Load radius and within the View distance draws its Far proxy, one Instance, in place of its Placements. Under it the Instance slots, the static TLAS and the flat cull keep M4's capacities (`docs/audit/m5/scalability.md`).
- **What it holds.** A first Primitive of merged, simplified geometry under one Material baked for it, windows included, and a second Primitive of the Chunk's ground tiles, unsimplified, under the ground's own Material. Only a scene with a Far field cooks proxies; it names its ground's Mesh.
- **Its Mesh.** The cooker merges the Chunk's static Placements and drops a Placement when every vertex of it lies within one pixel of one other Placement's surface, that Placement the Chunk's own or one of its eight neighbours', since a Placement belongs to the Chunk that holds its pivot. One pixel is its size at the Load radius at 1440p and a 45° field of view, 14.7 cm at 256 m. It welds what is left on position and simplifies it by edge collapse to one pixel of error, with no triangle count asked; the error is the simplifier's own measure, an approximation of the deviation [ref:meshoptimizer-0.25-simplify-error]. It refuses a proxy of more than 5,832 triangles outside its ground: the walls and roofs of `world`'s densest Chunk, 324 buildings of 18.
- **Its Material.** Two Channels, albedo and metallic-roughness, a texel two pixels at the Load radius, 29.5 cm, in one atlas: one rectangle a planar face of the first Primitive, with 2 texels of gutter a side on a grid of 4, dilated, so no chart bleeds into another down to mip 2 (amended 2026-11-18, [#904](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/904): at a texel a pixel, with 4 texels of gutter on a grid of 8, clean to mip 3, `world`'s bench segment passed the texture pool's Budget and two frame Budgets, and half the density is the fallback the decision's item 4 names; the gutter and the grid keep their metres, so each chart's padded size halves; on `world` every atlas then packs as before at half the side and no proxy's Mesh Blob changes, which the packer does not promise of every Chunk, as it orders charts by their rows in texels). The Chunk's Placements and its neighbours', those dropped from the geometry included, are rasterised into each chart along its normal with a depth test, the outermost surface winning, at 4 × 4 samples a texel, on the CPU.
- **Its Blobs.** A Mesh Blob per Chunk and, where its first Primitive has a triangle, a Material Blob and two Texture Blobs, each under an Asset ID made of the scene and the Chunk's coordinates, cooked together by `praxis cook <scene>` and by a dev launch that loads any of them while one is missing or stale. They share one Cook key, which covers the Chunk's and its neighbours' Cook keys, the Cook keys of the Meshes they place, of the Materials those draw with and of those Materials' albedo and metallic-roughness Textures, and the format and settings of each Blob. A proxy is fresh when its Mesh is in the Cooked cache under that key and, unless that Mesh names no Material, the three baked Blobs are too. A proxy whose sources name a Material or a Texture the cook did not read is kept as it is, as a Chunk is.
- **The swap.** A Far proxy shows exactly when its Chunk is not drawn, so the swap is at the Load radius, a whole Chunk at once, with no fade; a swap the owner sees on the first cooked proxies reopens the fade ([prototype](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/800#issuecomment-6875787005), item 3). A Late Chunk keeps its Far proxy until it loads: it shows coarse, never as a hole. A Far proxy entering at the View distance appears the same way, whole and at once.
- **Which are Resident.** A Far proxy is requested when its Chunk comes within the View distance and released past the View distance plus one Chunk's edge. It stays Resident while its Chunk is drawn, so leaving a Chunk never waits on a read.
- **Past the View distance.** Nothing is drawn but flat ground reaching the horizon, one Resident quad.

## Settled by the cook

The decisions left these to the build of the cook; the owner accepts them with this ADR.

- **The LOD chain.** A Far proxy is a Mesh and the culling pass selects its LOD as any Instance's. Its chain is cut on the welded positions, each coarser LOD simplified from LOD 0 to twice the error limit of the one before, and ends at eight LODs or at one that keeps more than 85% of the one before. A Mesh's own chain cannot serve. A face's vertices carry its normal and its UVs, and the corners of a box built face by face are vertices the simplifier locks [ref:meshoptimizer-0.25-vertex-kinds]. A collapse on welded positions leaves faces that need vertices of their own, since the simplifier only picks among the vertices it is given [ref:meshoptimizer-0.25-simplify-original-vertices], so each LOD's faces get flat normals and their own vertices, shared with a finer LOD where they are equal. Halving the triangles, as a Mesh's chain does, is not asked either: on `world`'s densest Chunk the first halving must break buildings, at a reported error of 2.79 m, where a pixel at the View distance spans 1.15 m. That Chunk's chain is LOD 0 at 5,832 triangles and LOD 1 at 3,240, its roofs flat and 0.3 m lower, which the simplifier reports as an error of 0.27 m.
- **The pivot.** A proxy's pivot is its Chunk's centre on the ground, and the cooker refuses a proxy with a vertex more than a Chunk's edge from it. ADR 0014 keeps every other cooked Mesh within 128 m of its pivot, and no pivot is within 128 m of a whole Chunk: its corners lie 181 m from its centre, and `world`'s farthest proxy vertex 190 m. The 128 m bound was set by the error of an f32 rotation, and a proxy is cooked to be placed unrotated. f32 resolves 0.015 mm between 128 and 256 m (derived, not measured through the shader), against a quarter of a pixel of 37 mm at the Load radius, the nearest a Far proxy is drawn unless its Chunk is late.
- **The ground is no surface to drop against.** A Placement that lies within a pixel of a ground tile is kept: the ground is not baked, so nothing would carry what was dropped onto it.
- **Dropped against the dropped.** A Placement is dropped against any other Placement, one that is itself dropped included, so two Placements each within a pixel of the other's surface both go. `world` places neither case.
- **Open land.** A Chunk with nothing but ground cooks a proxy whose first Primitive is empty.

## Settled by the bake

The bake decision left these to the build of the bake.

- **A chart.** A planar face is the triangles of the first Primitive that share vertices, which carry a position and a face normal. Its chart is the least-area rectangle about it along one of its edges, a texel two pixels at the Load radius, its rows no longer than its columns. Charts are packed on shelves, tallest first, each with 2 texels of gutter a side on a grid of 4, into the smallest square atlas of a power-of-two side that holds them; a proxy whose charts pass 8,192 texels a side fails its cook. `world`'s densest Chunk charts into 2048².
- **Coarser LODs.** Each face of a coarser LOD takes the chart nearest it among those facing within 45° of it, or among all of them where none does, its UVs held inside that chart. On `world`, LOD 1's roof tops read LOD 0's.
- **What a chart reads.** A source is a triangle of a Placement of the Chunk or of a neighbour that reaches it, one dropped from the geometry included, facing along the chart's normal within two pixels of its plane: the drop rule's pixel and the simplifier's, which hold a Placement dropped against one that is kept; one dropped against another that is itself dropped may lie farther out and is then not read. The outermost wins, at 4 × 4 samples a texel. A Placement the runtime does not draw, one without a Material whose Mesh has a Primitive with triangles that names none, is no source. A sample reads its source's Material as its factors times its albedo and metallic-roughness Textures, at the finest mip whose texels are no smaller than the sample, as the Texture cook filters it, so a roughness its normal Texture widens is read widened. A texel is the mean of its covered samples in linear light; one no source covers, a gutter's included, is dilated from its neighbours. A chart no source covers at all, and the texels outside every chart, hold the mean of the baked texels as stored, which mips past mip 2 blend in.
- **A source Texture's repeat is baked.** Where a face ends within half a texel, of the mip read, of the repeat's edge, its last texels blend in the far edge, as the source's own sampler does.
- **The baked Material.** Its factors are 1 for base colour, roughness and metallic, and it names no normal, occlusion or emissive Texture. Its Textures cook through the Texture cook, BC7 and BC5 with every mip, so they stream by mip as any Texture. The albedo atlas encodes at the BC7 encoder's `ultra_fast` profile where the Texture cook's is `basic`: measured at a texel a pixel, `world`'s atlases were 12.5 billion texels a Channel, baking every proxy took 7.5 core-hours under `basic` against 0.66 under `ultra_fast` (M4 Pro), and the profile moved a proxy's capture from the Load radius, on the four Chunks captured, in at most 21,790 of 3.7 million pixels, by 8 of 255 at most. Reopen on a proxy whose capture shows the encoder's blocks.
- **No area, no face.** A triangle without area has no normal to chart by and is left out of the proxy.

## Settled by the draw

The decisions left these to the build of the draw.

- **Which draws.** The Resident Far proxies are in the World beside the loaded Chunks, each a Chunk of one Placement that takes one Instance slot. A Far proxy is held, its slot an Instance with no Mesh, while it is past the View distance or its Chunk is drawn: loaded and within the Load radius. So the swap falls in the frame the Chunk's Placements are placed, once their BLASes are ready, and in the frame the Chunk is held or unloaded.
- **Its shadows.** A Far proxy's Mesh builds a BLAS from its LOD 0 at load, and the proxy is placed once that BLAS is ready, as a Chunk is. While drawn it holds one static TLAS entry under the mask bit an ordinary Instance has, so it casts and takes the sun's shadows, and a swap is a static change: the static TLAS takes both sides of it from its own next build, and the draw does not wait (ADR 0020).
- **Its mips.** Mip priority reads the drawn Far proxies beside the drawn Chunks' Placements, so a proxy's atlas streams its finer mips as its Chunk nears the Load radius.
- **Open land.** A Primitive without triangles needs no Material, so the proxy of a Chunk with nothing but ground draws its ground.
- **The ground past the View distance.** One of the ground's tiles, scaled to reach 1,024 km from the world's middle and laid 0.5 m under the tiles, placed by the scene as a Chunk of one Placement that is never held, and traced by no ray. It is not cut out under the Chunks and the Far proxies: their tiles lie over it and win the depth test, 0.5 m being 25 times the shallowest depth measured to hide it under them (`docs/performance.md`, "Measurements behind the code"). It also shows where a Far proxy is late. Its scale puts its corners past ADR 0014's 128 m, which bounds a cooked Mesh about its pivot for an f32 rotation's error: it is placed unrotated, f32 places a corner to 62.5 mm, and nothing nearer than the View distance shows it. From the camera's 60 m it ends a tenth of a pixel under the horizon at 1440p.

## Considered options

- Group proxies, one Mesh for several Chunks: at most 219 proxies are drawn, and their LOD chains already cut the triangles drawn with distance.
- Octahedral impostors: no streamed scene has trees or clutter, and one Instance per far object would bring the culling fallback with it. They wait for vegetation.
- Every Chunk's proxy Resident for the whole Game world: `world`'s buildings as bare boxes pass the geometry Budget, and the figure grows with content.
- The source Materials reused with small parts dropped, or vertex colours: `world`'s windows are 8 by 11 pixels at the swap and would vanish there.
- Vertex clustering and pruning of small components under a cap of 3,863 triangles: measured on `world`'s densest Chunk, they move an outline by 12 and 41 pixels. The same cap with the error let rise puts every roofline 2 pixels low at a hard swap.
- A drop rule by bounding size, what shipped engines use: a threshold that drops a window also drops a free-standing part 11 pixels tall, which no bake can carry.
- Voxel remeshing on a newer `meshopt`: nothing measured calls for it.
- The ground left out of the proxy and drawn by the flat ground past the View distance: it lies in the plane of loaded Chunks' ground tiles and would have to be kept from drawing under them, Chunk by Chunk. The ground baked into the proxy's atlas cost 3.02 M texels a Chunk of one colour at a texel a pixel, a quarter of that at two.

## Consequences

- ADR 0013's 2 km View distance is drawn this way, and the "merged far proxies" of ADR 0020 are these.
- ADR 0014's 128 m pivot bound exempts a Far proxy's Mesh, as *The pivot* above has it.
- The Material table counts Far proxies' Materials apart, and a frame's loads are capped by count (ADR 0017).
- A streamed scene whose Chunk keeps more triangles than the cap at one pixel fails its cook, naming the count.
- A LOD error is what the simplifier reports, which on `world`'s flattened roofs is a tenth under the distance they moved.
- A dev launch that loads a Far proxy hashes the albedo and metallic-roughness Textures its sources draw with, loaded or not, to find its Cook key, and bakes the proxy where a Blob of it is missing or stale (ADR 0018's Cook key clause, amended).
