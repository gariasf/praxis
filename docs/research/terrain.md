# Terrain under Praxis's ADRs: state of the art

Researched 2026-11-21 for ticket #948 (parent #944, the world-systems track). It surveys how a Skyrim- or RDR-class Game world's ground is drawn, traced, textured and streamed, against ADRs 0008, 0014, 0017, 0018, 0020 and 0026 and M5's heightfield check (`docs/audit/m5/scalability.md`, rows H01 to H08). It ends in options and chooses none; costs no source publishes are prototype questions. Vegetation (#949), sky, clouds and fog are out of scope.

**Summary.** Shipped open-world terrains draw a height field on regular grids or simplified meshes. Most morph between LODs in the vertex shader, most recent ones blend ground through a per-texel index map, and all add cliffs, caves and overhangs as ordinary meshes. None of that needs a tessellation stage or screen-space history. The open questions are what stands for terrain in the TLAS and what is drawn of it past 2 km.

- **Geometry.** Four forms run on what wgpu offers: a cooked Mesh per Chunk with a LOD chain (Call of Duty), grid patches selected on the GPU and morphed (Far Cry 5, O3DE, CDLOD, `bevy_terrain` in WGSL), clipmap rings (Terrain3D, Lumix), and adaptive bisection kept on the GPU (research). Battlefield 3, Fyrox and Torque3D stitch or skirt LODs without a morph. Nanite swaps under a pixel of error and lets TAA hide the rest.
- **Rays.** Shipped engines put terrain triangles in the acceleration structure (Far Cry 6, Assassin's Creed Shadows, O3DE; Unreal rebuilds Landscape's every frame because its LOD morphs), or march the height field after the BVH trace, bounded by its hit (Metro Exodus, Snowdrop). Triangles are fastest per ray on ray-tracing hardware: NVIDIA says so, and the one measurement against a custom intersection finds two orders of magnitude on average. A march costs no acceleration-structure bytes and nothing in a Fast travel's hold; no source times it alone.
- **Past the View distance.** Far Cry 5 keeps its 2 km root tiles resident; O3DE draws to 4,096 m. Relief past 2 km can cast without a far TLAS if shadow rays march a coarse height field. The alternative, a whole-world mesh in the static TLAS, overlaps every Instance; Lumen measured 44% on near rays from such overlap (PS5, 1080p).
- **Surface.** The index map is the shipped convention: material IDs a texel, the four nearest blended by height. Tiling is broken by per-cell rotation, a second scale with distance, or hex tiling (MIT demo, no precomputation). Virtual textures carry Far Cry 4 and 5 and Call of Duty at 8x anisotropy behind a 4-texel border, the limit ADR 0017 refused them for. A baked far colour is low mips or a coarse map, inside ADR 0017.
- **What other surveys may rely on.** A height query: f64 position in; height, normal, surface ID, or an explicit nothing for a hole or an unloaded Chunk out, interpolated on the collision's triangles. A collision shape: a static regular height field per Chunk at the drawn LOD 0's spacing, 16-bit samples, a hole and a material per cell, one diagonal. Jolt, PhysX, parry and Godot all take that form.
- **Cost of delay.** Every option rebuilds three things M5 decided (the Far proxy's cap and ground, flat ground past 2 km, its untraced shadow: H06 to H08), none before terrain is charted. Options that march touch one thing M6 is about to fix: the probe and shadow rays' trace function needs room for a second traversal. Two things bind early at no cost: a heightfield source format, which ADR 0011 and glTF lack, and a byte-identical cook for erosion.

Sources: papers and the foundational paper of each technique; GDC and SIGGRAPH Advances talks with speaker notes; the open engines the register (#946) lists for terrain, at its pinned commits; Unreal Engine 5.9, Unity 6000.0 with HDRP 17.0 and Flax through documentation only; the height-field shapes of Jolt 5.7.0, PhysX 5.13.0, parry 0.31.1 and Godot 4.7.2. Every claim relied on is in `docs/references.toml`, and every quote there was matched by script against a saved copy on 2026-11-21. Spartan Engine was opened only at `cb258a1`, its last MIT commit; `godot_voxel`'s Transvoxel tables were not opened. Nothing was measured here.

Licence classes (#944, Notes): **A** adaptable with citation, **E** evidence only, **P** Apache-2.0 alone. Temporal: **none** (no history, or the source says so), **TAA** (the images or method lean on TAA, a temporal upscaler or refinement over frames), **–** (not stated).

## Geometry and LOD

**1. A Mesh per Chunk.** The heightfield check sized this as its plainest form, 131,072 triangles a Chunk at 1 m (H01 to H06). Call of Duty draws grids at a quadtree's leaves and simplified meshes above, storing X and Y and reading Z from the height map [ref:etienne-2023-call-of-duty-terrain-geometry]. Treyarch reports 50,000 triangles against a clipmap's 170,000 "and the same visual quality", and morphs each vertex to a stored coarser position before the swap [ref:hooker-2021-call-of-duty-mesh-lod]. Unreal's Experimental Mesh Partition simplifies a static mesh per partition unit, with collision and far-field meshes beside it [ref:ue-5.9-mesh-partition-transformers]. In Praxis it is the Mesh path as is, but a Chunk-wide Instance draws one LOD, so finer selection needs patches (H03: 16 m patches over 12 Chunks take 3,072 static TLAS entries). M5's hard swap under a pixel would hold; Nanite names what hides the rest, "temporal antialiasing" [ref:nanite-taa-lod], and Unreal leaves Nanite Landscape's distant seam holes to be "corrected by temporal anti-aliasing" [ref:ue-5.9-nanite-landscape]. A morph needs a second position a vertex.

**2. Grid patches selected on the GPU and morphed.** CDLOD selects quadtree nodes by 3D distance and morphs each node into the coarser grid in the vertex shader before the swap [ref:strugar-2010-cdlod-cost], [ref:strugar-2010-cdlod-morph]; very rough terrain needs per-dataset settings [ref:strugar-2010-cdlod-limits]. Far Cry 5 selects and culls 16 × 16-vertex patches in compute into one indirect draw, about 0.1 ms, welding edges to coarser neighbours [ref:moore-2018-far-cry-5-gpu-patch-pipeline], [ref:moore-2018-far-cry-5-lod-stitching]. O3DE draws sectors to 4,096 m, LOD 0 to 128 m, with a 16 m morph band [ref:o3de-2610.0-terrain-mesh-defaults], [ref:o3de-2610.0-terrain-morph]. `bevy_terrain` refines tiles in WGSL compute into one indirect draw with CDLOD's morph [ref:bevy-terrain-5a88daf-refine], [ref:bevy-terrain-5a88daf-morph]; on an Apple M1 its prepass was bound by dispatch overhead [ref:kuehnert-2022-thesis-measurements]. Unreal's Landscape morphs in the vertex shader [ref:ue-5.9-landscape-overview]; Battlefield 3 stitched by index permutations, vertices unchanged [ref:widmark-2012-battlefield-3-terrain-mesh]. In Praxis it is a second draw path, and as the largest occluder it joins the Hi-Z culling's first phase. Normals taken per pixel from the height texture keep shading independent of the geometry LOD [ref:terrain3d-1.1.0-normals].

**3. Clipmap rings.** Nested grids follow the viewer and morph near each level's edge by its position, not by time [ref:losasso-2004-geometry-clipmaps-idea], [ref:losasso-2004-geometry-clipmaps-cost]. Terrain3D instances a few flat meshes, re-placed whenever the camera moves one vertex spacing, and geomorphs over each ring's outer half [ref:terrain3d-1.1.0-mesher], [ref:terrain3d-1.1.0-geomorph], seven LODs at 1 m by default [ref:terrain3d-1.1.0-mesh-defaults]. Its documented flicker came from TAA and motion vectors fed by the moving rings [ref:terrain3d-1.1.0-temporal]. Lumix draws rings with no vertex buffer [ref:lumix-8ce5ab5-terrain-rings]. Whatever stands for a ring in the TLAS cannot be the drawn mesh.

**4. Adaptive bisection on the GPU.** A concurrent binary tree keeps a longest-edge-bisection triangulation in one buffer, updated by compute with atomics and drawn by one indirect call [ref:dupuy-2020-cbt-method]: under 5 ms at full HD on an RTX 2080, 6 m precision over 52 km [ref:dupuy-2020-cbt-cost-limits]. Unity's prototype needs only compute and indirect draws [ref:deliot-2021-unity-cbt-terrain]. The 2024 form pools 128k bisectors over any mesh in 7 MiB [ref:benyoub-2024-cbt-large-scale-method] and updates in 0.084 to 0.1 ms on an AMD 6800 XT [ref:benyoub-2024-cbt-large-scale-cost]; its authors trace it by "a full rebuild every frame of the tlas and blas" [ref:benyoub-2024-cbt-course-slides], which on the M4 Pro is the heightfield check's 2.34 to 2.91 ms for 131,072 triangles (derived). It splits one level per update with no morph named, so without history a split shows. libcbt is MIT or Unlicense [ref:libcbt-2d5439a-parallel-update].

**5. Tessellation and its kin.** Wildlands tessellated near ground to a triangle every 2 cm at "a lot of GPU time" [ref:werle-2017-wildlands-terrain]; Far Cry 5 rejected tessellated displacement for cost [ref:moore-2018-far-cry-5-cliffs-and-near-displacement]; Call of Duty uses it only where enabled [ref:etienne-2023-call-of-duty-holes-displacement]. wgpu has no tessellation stage and experimental mesh shaders (`docs/research/wgpu-capability-matrix.md`); the one mesh-shader terrain talk was read as an abstract [ref:englert-2020-mesh-shader-terrain]; displacement micromaps are withdrawn [ref:nvidia-2025-displacement-micromap-archived].

## Rays

**6. Triangles in the TLAS.** Far Cry 6 keeps clipmapped terrain in the TLAS beyond its objects' radius [ref:brenham-2022-far-cry-6-terrain-clipmap-in-tlas]; Assassin's Creed Shadows updates terrain patches around the camera [ref:leblanc-2025-ac-shadows-terrain-patches-in-bvh]; O3DE gives every sector of every LOD a ray-tracing mesh of 32-bit floats, swapped as raster selection changes [ref:o3de-2610.0-terrain-ray-tracing-lods], [ref:o3de-2610.0-terrain-ray-tracing-vertices]; Unreal rebuilds Landscape every frame "to support its continuously morphing level of detail" [ref:ue-5.9-ray-tracing-landscape]. NVIDIA: use triangles when possible [ref:nvidia-2022-rtx-best-practices-triangles]. ADR 0020's static TLAS rebuilds only on static changes, so it holds a static BLAS per Chunk at one spacing: at 2 m, 1,490,944 compacted bytes and 0.98 to 1.22 ms of build in the load frame, within the Budget while the Arenas hold 56 (H02, H05); at 1 m, past it. The drawn and the traced surface then differ by up to a LOD's error, and a ray leaving the drawn surface below the traced one shadows itself.

**7. A height field marched after the TLAS query.** Metro Exodus marches the height map in ray generation and shortens the ray to the hit, "almost free" by latency hiding, within a ray-tracing stage of 1 to 6 ms on an RTX 2080 at 2560 × 1440 [ref:shyshkovtsov-2019-metro-exodus-terrain-heightmap-in-raygen]. Snowdrop traces the BVH, then marches "using trace hit as maxT" [ref:kuenlin-2024-snowdrop-terrain-marched-after-bvh], [ref:koshlo-2024-snowdrop-rt-world]; Lumen's software path marches a heightfield per Landscape component [ref:wright-2022-lumen-landscape-heightfield-software-tracing]. The traversal is a maximum mipmap: 49 frames a second at 4,096² against 9 by uniform steps, GeForce 8800 Ultra, 1024 × 768 [ref:tevs-2008-maximum-mipmaps-cost]; about 30 over a 56 × 85 km elevation model, GTX 280, 1920 × 1080 [ref:dick-2009-terrain-raycasting]; sun shadows in a 5.4 ms frame, GTX 970M, 1920 × 1080 [ref:jung-2020-terrain-soft-shadows-maximum-mipmaps]. OGRE-Next marches toward the sun in compute when the light moves [ref:ogre-next-c13fa29-terra-shadow], [ref:ogre-next-c13fa29-terra-shadow-update]. In Praxis the march runs in the shadow mask and probe rays, and terrain never reaches the TLAS or its Budget. The shadow mask was cut to one ray-query call a surface because ray-query code costs by its presence (`docs/performance.md`, "Where the shadow mask pass's time goes", "One ray-query call a surface"); a march adds a loop after that call.

**8. Procedural primitives.** wgpu 30 added BLAS AABB geometry, intersected on Metal (`docs/research/wgpu-capability-matrix.md`, After 29.0.1). Tessellation-free displacement mapping traverses a min-max mipmap in an intersection shader, and its authors find triangles "two orders of magnitude faster on average", 103 against 1.8 million rays a second in one scene on an RTX 2080 Max-Q [ref:thonat-2021-tfdm-custom-intersection-vs-triangles]. Its successor still calls triangles "the method of choice" for static content [ref:thonat-2023-rmip-custom-intersection-vs-triangles].

**9. A coarse proxy in the same TLAS.** Lumen traces near rays to 200 m, then merged HLOD meshes; with both in one TLAS near rays took 2.37 ms against 1.65, and 1.77 once the far set was moved away (PS5, 1080p), and its authors call a second TLAS "a proper solution" [ref:wright-2022-lumen-far-field-proxies-in-one-tlas]. Unreal extends its ray-traced far field to a kilometre on HLOD meshes [ref:ue-5.9-ray-tracing-landscape]. Praxis traces Far proxies to 2 km already (ADR 0026); H08's whole-world mesh would add one entry of 9,318,400 bytes whose box overlaps every Instance.

**10. Shadow textures and horizon maps.** The Witcher 3 stores the greatest height in shadow per texel of a clipmap, rebuilt as it streams or the time of day changes, in 10 MB plus 10 MB [ref:gollent-2014-witcher-3-terrain-shadows-clipmap]. Red Dead Redemption 2 marches toward the sun into a 128 × 128 texture refined over frames, for its volumetrics [ref:bauer-2019-rdr2-terrain-shadow-map]. Horizon maps store each texel's horizon by direction [ref:sloan-2000-interactive-horizon-mapping]; the 2025 Fourier form costs 2.8 ms over none (RTX 2050, 1920 × 1080) and is defined for height-map surfaces [ref:fritsch-2025-horizon-maps], [ref:fritsch-2025-horizon-map-limits-and-artefacts]. None is a ray query, so each amends ADR 0020; row 7 reaches as far by a ray.

## Past the View distance

**11. Coarse relief to the horizon.** Far Cry 5's 2 km roots stay resident, each with a 129 × 129 16-bit height map, a baked normal and albedo, from which far terrain is shaded [ref:moore-2018-fc5-terrain-quadtree], [ref:moore-2018-far-cry-5-always-resident-root-payload]. Far Cry 4 used offline-baked geometry and textures past 300 m [ref:farcry4-2015-vista-terrain]. Terrain3D draws its rings over shader noise without collision where no data exists [ref:terrain3d-1.1.0-world-background]. H07 sizes the whole world at 32 m posts at 204,800 triangles, within a pixel at 2 km for relief of 222 m radius of curvature or more. In options 2 to 4 below, the grid's coarse levels are this mesh; its shadow past 2 km is row 7 or row 9.

## Surface

**12. Weight splat maps.** Bloom's 2000 note draws a pass per material per chunk through a 64 × 64 alpha texture [ref:bloom-2000-splatting]. Unity allows four layers a pass, each pass adding time, eight in one under HDRP [ref:unity-6000.0-terrain-layers-per-pass]; Unreal adds a weightmap every four layers [ref:ue-5.9-landscape-layer-blend]. HTerrain's four-weight shader costs 0.8 ms full screen on a GeForce 1060 and its 16-texture form 1.8 ms [ref:hterrain-49887ff-texturing]. Cost grows with layers.

**13. An index map with a height blend.** Far Cry 5 stores an 8-bit material ID a texel, reads the four nearest and blends them by height-modified bilinear weights, 16 samples a pixel a projection, 32 of about 150 materials resident in about 70 MB [ref:farcry5-2018-splat-shading]. Wildlands shipped 143 materials, 32 cached in a texture array behind an indirection table [ref:wildlands-2017-materials-per-pixel]. Call of Duty moved from 32 alpha layers, all of which could shade one pixel, to one index a vertex and up to 256 layers [ref:cod-2023-ompv], its index mips point-sampled [ref:cod-2023-index-map-mips]. The Witcher 3 paints two textures a texel, blended by the background's slope [ref:witcher3-2014-control-map]. Terrain3D packs two IDs, a blend, rotation, scale and a hole bit in 32 bits [ref:terrain3d-1.1.0-control-map], 5 to 20 lookups a pixel [ref:terrain3d-1.1.0-blend-cost]; O3DE holds two IDs and a blend a texel [ref:o3de-2610.0-terrain-material-blend]. A height blend adds each layer's height to its weight [ref:mishkinis-2013-height-blend], [ref:ue-5.9-landscape-layer-blend]; Spartan found a hard gate "flickered with taa" [ref:spartan-engine-cb258a1-terrain-height-blend]. In Praxis an index map is a Texture per Chunk with point-sampled mips, and layers need the Material form #17's materials line says no five-Channel Material holds. Chunk seams need shared edge texels [ref:bloom-2000-chunk-seams]; texture arrays, which ADR 0017's classes are, need no padding [ref:frostbite-2007-atlas-padding].

**14. Tiling breakers.** Histogram-preserving blending of three offset fetches costs 0.29 ms full screen on a GTX 980 at 1920 × 1080, fails on patterned textures, and keeps anisotropy through explicit gradients [ref:heitz-neyret-2018-cost], [ref:heitz-neyret-2018-limits-filtering]; its practical form is 0.182 ms against 0.039 plain for 1,024² DXT1 [ref:deliot-heitz-2019-cost]. It samples a Gaussianised copy, which changes what the cooker stores; Burley builds its tables on load [ref:burley-2019-histogram-blending]. Hex tiling samples the source itself and blends normals as height derivatives [ref:mikkelsen-2022-hex-tiling]; its MIT demo keeps precision far from the origin [ref:mikkelsen-hextile-demo-licence-and-notes]; Spartan uses it with a 1,200 m cut-off [ref:spartan-engine-cb258a1-terrain-hex-biplanar]. Shipped measures are per-cell rotation with rotated derivatives (Call of Duty pays four samples where one did) and a second scale with distance [ref:terrain3d-1.1.0-detiling], [ref:cod-2023-tile-hiding-vista-uvs]. A per-tile hash aliases at terrain's minification [ref:quilez-texture-repetition]; Frostbite set Wang tiles aside as hard to mipmap [ref:frostbite-2007-wang-tiles]. Stochastic texture filtering resolves its noise with DLSS [ref:fajardo-2023-stochastic-texture-filtering], so ranks last under ADR 0008.

**15. Slopes.** Triplanar blends three projections by the normal [ref:geiss-2007-triplanar]; biplanar keeps two, gradients taken before the axis choice [ref:quilez-biplanar]. Far Cry 5's triplanar cliffs took 48 samples, 96 with a second scale, cut to 8 and 12 by picking a material and a projection per pixel with a noise it calls "quite stable" and free of aliasing [ref:farcry5-2018-cliffs-dual-scale-stochastic]. Terrain3D projects steep ground on one of eight vertical planes [ref:terrain3d-1.1.0-slope-projection].

**16. A virtual texture or texture clipmap.** Battlefield 3 composited terrain into a virtual texture, listing "Limited hardware filtering support" among its issues [ref:widmark-2012-bf3-terrain-virtual-texture], [ref:bf3-2012-vt-issues]. Far Cry 4's adaptive form reaches 10 texels a centimetre over 10 km [ref:gdc-2015-far-cry-4-avt], at 8x anisotropy behind a 4-texel border with trilinear in software [ref:farcry4-2015-avt-filtering]; on PS4 its upkeep is 0.2 ms CPU and 0.1 ms GPU primed, 0.5 to 1 ms each while compositing, in 220 MB [ref:farcry4-2015-avt-cost]. Wildlands replaced its costly per-pixel blend with one [ref:wildlands-2017-virtual-texture]; Far Cry 5 caches its splat result there [ref:farcry5-2018-virtual-texture-cache]; Call of Duty keeps 4-pixel borders [ref:cod-2023-avt] and reports pages taking frames, or seconds on slow storage, to arrive [ref:cod-2023-vt-latency]. Wicked's version works around Metal's sparse textures [ref:wicked-engine-fc8385b-terrain-virtual-texture]; O3DE's optional texture clipmaps are uncompressed levels repainted by compute as the camera moves [ref:o3de-2610.0-terrain-clipmap-config], [ref:o3de-2610.0-terrain-clipmap-update]; Unreal's is a shading cache for static objects [ref:ue-5.9-runtime-virtual-texture]. Anisotropy is bounded by twice the border [ref:vanwaveren-2012-anisotropy-borders], ADR 0017's reason for refusing software virtual texturing.

**17. A baked far colour.** Bloom fades splats over a base texture baked from the same weights at 100 to 200 m, so colours match by construction [ref:bloom-2000-base-texture-fade]. Far Cry 5 shades far terrain from a 132 × 132 BC1 albedo a tile, blending five shading paths [ref:moore-2018-fc5-baked-albedo], [ref:farcry5-2018-near-far-blend]; The Witcher 3 streams a 12 MB colour clipmap [ref:witcher3-2014-colour-clipmap]; Call of Duty bakes its virtual texture's top six mips offline [ref:cod-2023-offline-baked-mips]; Torque3D and Unity draw a base texture past a distance [ref:torque3d-9521d43-terrain-base-texture], [ref:unity-6000.0-terrain-base-map]. Meshes join the ground by reading its height and colour [ref:cod-2023-skirts], [ref:farcry5-2018-mesh-terrain-blend]. In Praxis the far colour is a Texture streamed by mip; ADR 0026 priced the ground in the Far proxy's atlas at 3.02 M texels a Chunk at a texel a pixel.

## Shape past a height field

**18. Holes.** Far Cry 5 masks holes at 1 bit and outputs NaN positions, "not specifically documented in graphics APIs", for 1 m holes on 0.5 m posts [ref:moore-2018-far-cry-5-holes]; O3DE writes a non-finite position too [ref:o3de-2610.0-terrain-holes]; Call of Duty bakes cut-out volumes to 1 bit a vertex [ref:etienne-2023-call-of-duty-holes-displacement]. Holes reach collision in Terrain3D, Unity and Flax [ref:terrain3d-1.1.0-limits], [ref:unity-6000.0-terrain-holes], [ref:flax-docs-terrain-collision-holes]. A cooked grid can drop the triangles instead of relying on NaN.

**19. Meshes beside the height field.** Far Cry 5 generates cliff meshes that run the terrain shader and gives decals matching meshes [ref:moore-2018-far-cry-5-cliffs-and-near-displacement], [ref:farcry5-2018-mesh-terrain-blend]. Terrain3D and Godot send tunnels and caves to separate meshes [ref:terrain3d-1.1.0-limits], [ref:godot-4.7.2-heightmap-shape]. Unreal's Experimental Mesh Terrain drops the height field for meshes with overhangs and non-uniform resolution [ref:unreal-5.9-mesh-terrain], baking vertex attributes to a texture array [ref:ue-5.9-mesh-terrain-attributes], with a ray-tracing proxy option in 5.9 [ref:unreal-5.9-preview-release-notes]. In Praxis these are Placements; option 1 makes terrain the same kind.

**20. Voxels and near displacement.** Voxels hold caves at "far greater numbers of vertices and triangles", with visible LOD changes; geomorphing was set aside at 12 bytes a vertex [ref:lengyel-2010-voxel-terrain-dissertation]. `godot_voxel` admits "sharp little steps" at Transvoxel seams and fades LODs by a screen-space hash discard [ref:godot-voxel-1.7-lod-fade], [ref:godot-voxel-1.7-sdf], the dithered fade M5 ruled out (#758). Without tessellation, near displacement is parallax occlusion mapping, 8 to 40 samples a pixel with no true silhouette [ref:tatarchuk-2006-parallax-occlusion-mapping], which HDRP's ray tracing excludes [ref:unity-hdrp-17.0-ray-tracing-limits], or denser displaced rings at "a potentially significant performance cost" [ref:terrain3d-1.1.0-displacement].

## Where it comes from

**21. Elevation, erosion and generators.** Fetchable real elevation exists (`docs/research/world-content-sources.md`); 3DEP serves cloud-optimized GeoTIFF [ref:usgs-3dep-products]. Unreal imports 16-bit PNG, R16 and RAW with a JSON sidecar [ref:ue-5.9-landscape-heightmap-format], Unity 16-bit RAW [ref:unity-6000.0-terrain-heightmap-raw]. glTF's registry holds no height field [ref:gltf-5b00b91-extension-registry], so ADR 0011 leaves the format open; Praxis's `image` crate reads PNG, and GeoTIFF would need a dependency or a converter. Generators are procedural, erosion or example-based [ref:galin-2019-terrain-modeling-review]. Schott and colleagues released MIT code for stream-power erosion from a painted uplift map [ref:schott-2023-stream-power-erosion-code] and amplification alternating erosion with ×2 upsampling in OpenGL compute [ref:schott-2024-multi-scale-erosion-code]. A learned generator runs at 0.66 s steady state on an RTX 3090 Ti, its author conceding noise's speed [ref:goslin-2025-terrain-diffusion]. Spartan erodes in its cook with droplets and thermal steps [ref:spartan-engine-cb258a1-erosion], independent of thread count through fixed chunks, seeds and summation order [ref:spartan-engine-cb258a1-erosion-determinism], and caches the result [ref:spartan-engine-cb258a1-terrain-physics]. ADR 0018 wants the same bytes everywhere, and the cooked Mesh hash tests have met glam's SIMD rounding and platform sine and cosine (`CLAUDE.md`, review checklist): erosion in the cook runs on the CPU in a fixed order without platform transcendentals, or its output is committed as source.

Per Chunk, in every option, the cooker would emit a height grid sharing edge posts with its neighbours, quantised to 16 bits over the Chunk's range as Jolt does per block [ref:jolt-5.7.0-heightfield-shape]; a hole mask; an index map; the collision shape; the far field's coarse level; and for option 1 the Meshes, each under an Asset ID of scene and Chunk as ADR 0026's proxies are.

## The engines register's entries

Evidence, not templates; classes as the register gives them (`docs/research/open-engines-register.md`).

- **O3DE** (A): morphed sector grids, ray-tracing meshes per LOD following raster selection, optional texture clipmaps, an index map, the fullest query interface. Nearest option 2, but its tracing changes the TLAS as the camera moves.

- **Spartan `cb258a1`** (A): procedural layers, hex tiling, biplanar, soft height blend, deterministic erosion. Evidence for rows 13 to 15 and 21; its tessellation has no counterpart here.

- **Torque3D** (A): cells by screen error, skirts [ref:torque3d-9521d43-terrain-lod-skirts]. **Lumix** (A): rings, an index blend over a satellite texture past 50 m [ref:lumix-8ce5ab5-terrain-texturing]. **HTerrain** (A): three texturing schemes, collision from the height map [ref:hterrain-49887ff-collision].

## What each geometry option puts in the TLAS and past the View distance

- **Mesh per Chunk** (1): a static BLAS per Chunk at a fixed LOD; past the Load radius the Far proxy's relief (H06's cap raised); past 2 km a whole-world coarse Mesh, traced as one entry (row 9) or marched (row 7).
- **Morphed grid** (2) and **rings** (3): nothing drawn can be traced; a static BLAS per Chunk at a fixed spacing from the same heights, or a march. The grid's coarse levels draw relief past the Load radius and 2 km; Far proxies keep only Placements.

## What other surveys may rely on

A **height query** reads the cooked height grid of a Chunk loaded within the hold radius, on the CPU and in the fixed schedule (ADR 0022); the cooker places things with the same function.

- In: an f64 world position (ADR 0014) and a sampler. O3DE offers bilinear, clamped to the grid, and exact [ref:o3de-2610.0-terrain-query-point]; here the default reads the collision's triangles, as O3DE's ray cast matches its physics' diagonal [ref:o3de-2610.0-terrain-raycast].
- Out: nothing for a hole or a missing Chunk, never an invented height, as Terrain3D's NaN, Fyrox's `Option` and Jolt's `false` [ref:terrain3d-1.1.0-collision-holes], [ref:fyrox-1.0.0-terrain-queries], [ref:jolt-5.7.0-heightfield-queries]; otherwise an f64 height, a smooth normal and the dominant layer's surface ID.
- Batch forms over a list and a region, and a ray form, as O3DE's [ref:o3de-2610.0-terrain-query-batch]; Terrain3D has single points only [ref:terrain3d-1.1.0-queries], `bevy_terrain` takes a `DVec3` [ref:bevy-terrain-5a88daf-height-query], Unity clamps to the terrain [ref:unity-6000.0-terrain-sample-height].
- The same grid as a Texture for shaders (water, rain cover, meshes meeting the ground; row 17).

A **collision shape** is a static regular height field per Chunk in its own f32 frame, origin in f64.

- Posts at the drawn LOD 0's spacing, as O3DE and Terrain3D build theirs [ref:o3de-2610.0-terrain-collider], [ref:terrain3d-1.1.0-collision-shape]; Unreal and Flax document coarser collision as a choice [ref:ue-5.9-landscape-collision-mip], [ref:flax-docs-terrain-collision-holes].
- 16-bit samples: Jolt compresses 1 to 16 bits relative to each block [ref:jolt-5.7.0-heightfield-shape]; PhysX stores signed 16-bit heights [ref:physx-5.13.0-heightfield-sample].
- A hole and a material index per cell (Jolt `FLT_MAX` and up to 256 materials, PhysX's hole material, parry's removed triangles, Godot's NaN) [ref:parry-0.31.1-heightfield], [ref:godot-4.7.2-heightmap-shape].
- One diagonal, the drawn mesh's; PhysX and parry can flip it per cell, O3DE's collider fixes it.
- Static: Jolt's shape "Cannot be used as a dynamic object" and races if edited while queried [ref:jolt-5.7.0-heightfield-shape].
- Loaded on collision's own radius, which `docs/research/living-open-worlds.md` finds wider than the Load radius.

Jolt, Godot (A), PhysX and parry (P) all take this form, so the vehicles survey (#952) chooses an engine without changing it; caves collide as their own meshes.

## Options

Each geometry option carries its rays and far field; the surface choice fits any.

1. **Cooked terrain Meshes.** A Mesh per Chunk from the cooked grid, simplified above LOD 0 (row 1), through the culling pass and Meshlets with M5's hard swap; a morph is a variant that changes the cooked vertex. A static BLAS per Chunk at 2 m. Far proxies carry relief; past 2 km a whole-world Mesh at 32 m posts is drawn and traced as one static entry.
2. **A morphed grid, traced as triangles.** Patches selected in compute into one indirect draw, morphed in the vertex shader, heights in a Texture per Chunk, normals per pixel (row 2). A static BLAS per Chunk at 2 m within the Load radius. Coarse levels draw relief beyond, the coarsest resident for the whole world, and Far proxies lose their ground Primitive; relief past the static TLAS is a whole-world coarse Mesh in it (row 9).
3. **Option 2, marched past the Load radius.** Rays that miss the TLAS march a coarse whole-world height field, bounded by the TLAS hit (row 7): relief past 2 km casts with no far TLAS and no BLAS bytes.
4. **Option 2, marched everywhere.** No terrain in the TLAS (Metro Exodus, Snowdrop). H02's Budget and H05's hold are untouched; all of terrain's ray cost is shader loops.
5. **Adaptive bisection** (row 4), traced by a per-frame BLAS or a march. Research, splits without a morph.

Surface: **S1**, index map, height blend, hex tiling or per-cell rotation, biplanar slopes, baked far colour, inside ADR 0017; **S2**, S1 cached in a virtual texture or clipmap, which reopens ADR 0017. In every option: holes in the grid, cliffs and caves as Placements; voxels rank last. Source: 16-bit greyscale PNG with a sidecar for spacing, height range and origin, as Unreal and Unity import, or GeoTIFF behind a converter or a new dependency (an owner decision).

## Cost of delay

**Would rebuild a decision M5 took: three in every option, none before terrain.** ADR 0026's Far proxy cap and 8-triangle ground (H06), flat ground past 2 km (H07) and its untraced shadow (H08) change the Far proxy Blob and re-run its cook. Options 1 to 3 also spend the acceleration structures' Budget and lengthen a Fast travel's hold with a BLAS per Chunk (H02, H05, on #851's path): both survive at 2 m, neither at 1 m on the M4 Pro. Option 1's morph changes every terrain Mesh's cooked vertex, a re-cook if done after content. Option 4 touches neither H02 nor H05.

**Would rebuild a decision M6 is about to take: one, for options 3 and 4.** M6 fixes the probe rays' trace function and its Budget. A march bounded by the TLAS hit is a second traversal there and in the shadow mask, on hardware where ray-query code costs by its presence (`docs/performance.md`); leaving the seam costs nothing at M6. Options 1 and 2 touch nothing of M6. In every option, M6's ray costs on flat ground omit terrain's occlusion.

**Only adds beside.** Options 2 to 5 add a draw path that joins the Hi-Z first phase as an occluder (ADR 0019). S1 adds an index Texture per Chunk and the layered Material #17 already reserves. The height query, collision shape and per-Chunk cook add beside but fix the Chunk Blob (ADR 0018), cheapest settled with the world-content Reference scene. S2 reopens ADR 0017.

**Binds early, at no cost now.** A heightfield source format, since the world-content Reference scene waits on a heightfield import (#944) and ADR 0011 names only glTF; and a determinism rule for erosion in the cook (ADR 0018).

## Costs no source publishes: prototype questions

Each on the M4 Pro and the RTX 2070 SUPER from one engine source (ADR 0013). "`world` with relief" is `world` with its ground replaced by a 10.24 km tile of real elevation (`docs/research/world-content-sources.md`).

1. **A hard swap without history.** Does a Mesh-per-Chunk terrain switching LOD under a pixel show in motion at 4x MSAA, against the same terrain morphed? Judged by the owner on `world` with relief; it decides option 1 against the rest.
2. **The march.** Shadow-mask and probe-ray time with a maximum-mipmap march after the TLAS query, bounded by its hit, for relief past 2 km (option 3) and for all terrain (option 4). On `world` with relief and on `town` over a height field.
3. **A BLAS per Chunk.** The load frame and a Fast travel's hold with a 32,768-triangle terrain BLAS a Chunk on #851's path, against H05's derived 29.6 to 33.4 ms. On `world` with relief.
4. **Drawn against traced.** The ray offset a morphed grid needs over a static 2 m BLAS to stop shadowing itself, and what it leaks under a sun at 2° to 10°. On `world` with relief.
5. **The ground's shading.** Lit-pass time at 1440p, 4x MSAA, for four index texels of two layers each with height blend, hex tiling and biplanar slopes, against one Material. On `world` with relief.
6. **Relief past 2 km.** Near-field shadow-ray time with a 32 m whole-world Mesh as one static entry (option 2) against the march (option 3). On `world` with relief.
