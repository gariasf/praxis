# LOD, HLOD and the far field without TAA: state of the art

Researched 2026-11-06 for ticket #627, survey 3 of map #609 and M4's exit literature scan. M5 charts the far field from it: LOD, HLOD, far proxies, culling at millions of Instances and the 2 km view.

**Summary.** Discrete LOD is the settled technique: Unreal, Godot and niagara cook a chain with a simplifier and keep each level's error, and every engine checked picks a level by projected size against a threshold, which niagara does per draw in its GPU culling pass. `meshopt` 0.6.2, already a dependency, provides the simplifier, the error and the selection formula. What no source settles is the switch without temporal history. Cesium's dithered fade for Unreal rests on a node meant for TAA and fails under MSAA, and Godot says its noise shows less with TAA; Unity and Bevy use a static screen-space pattern and say nothing of its look without TAA. No shipped title was found that cross-fades LODs per sample under MSAA. Plain alpha-to-coverage cannot cross-fade two LODs, because alpha draws its samples from one nested family, so the two levels' masks overlap instead of complementing; complementary `sample_mask` writes can, untested anywhere published. Unreal's HLOD layers default to 256 m cells, each HLOD swapped hard against its cell; octahedral impostors blend their views within the frame and need no history. At millions of Instances, shipped renderers cull cells first and Instances inside the survivors; Praxis's flat cull costs about 4 to 40 times the published per-Instance figures (the 40 against Nanite's best) before any hierarchy. Persistent-thread traversal rests on a forward-progress guarantee that neither Metal nor WebGPU gives and that Apple's A12 and A14 GPUs failed in a published test. `town`'s wires stop breaking up when widened to a pixel with fading coverage, as phone-wire AA and hair renderers do. The note ends in options for M5 and chooses none.

Sources: SIGGRAPH Advances talks (Haar and Aaltonen 2015, Karis 2021, Frostbite 2019), GDC and GCAP decks (Guerrilla 2017, Ubisoft 2018, Epic 2024), REAC 2021 (Activision), papers from 1996 to 2021, the WebGPU and Metal Shading Language specifications, Unreal Engine 5.8.4, Unity Graphics, Godot 4.4 and 4.5, Bevy 0.20.1, niagara and meshoptimizer source, vendor and studio documentation. Each claim relied on is in `docs/references.toml`, cited by key. Boundaries: the TLAS for far proxies and which LOD each BLAS holds are survey 4's (`docs/research/acceleration-structures-for-streamed-worlds.md`, rows 11 to 14), summarised in row 17; Chunk streaming is survey 2's. Ruled out by the ticket: virtual geometry that relies on TAA (ADRs 0008, 0019).

## Where Praxis stands

`town`'s geometry passes sit at their no-reuse vertex-fetch floor, so drawing half the indices takes about 2.8 ms off its 8.6 ms gate (1.1 ms depth pre-pass, 1.4 ms lit pass, 0.3 ms culling; `docs/audit/m3.5/performance.md`, option I). The culling pass tests every Instance slot, 4.5 ns a slot on the M4 Pro, so the 3.14 M Instances a 2 km view holds at `town`'s density would take 14.1 ms (`docs/audit/m3.5/scalability.md`, R09). At 1440p with Praxis's 45° vertical field of view a pixel spans 0.575 mrad, so `town`'s 3 cm wire is one pixel wide at 52 m and a quarter pixel at 209 m, and a 1 m object at 2 km covers 0.87 px. The main pass runs 4x MSAA with alpha-to-coverage off. With shadow rays on, its depth pre-pass writes each sample's surface normal in `fs_surface_normals`; with them off it has no fragment stage, and phase two's depth draw in the lit pass runs the empty `fs_depth`. The lit pass already reads `@builtin(sample_mask)` as input.

## Generating and choosing levels

**1. Discrete LOD chains.** Quadric error metrics contract vertex pairs and track error in a 10-float quadric per vertex [ref:garland-1997-qem]; Hoppe's improved quadric adds appearance attributes such as normals and colours to the error [ref:hoppe-1999-attribute-quadrics], as meshoptimizer's attribute-aware mode does, and its permissive mode trades seam preservation for quality [ref:meshoptimizer-0.25-attribute-simplify]. meshoptimizer targets an error normalized to the mesh's extent [ref:meshoptimizer-0.25-simplify-error] and returns each level's error [ref:meshoptimizer-0.25-lod-selection], and recommends simplifying each level from the previous one and accumulating the errors [ref:meshoptimizer-0.25-lod-chain]. Chains in practice: Unreal's LOD Group presets keep 50% of the triangles a level; Small Prop has 4 levels at 10 pixels of error, High Detail 6 at 6 [ref:ue-5.6-auto-lod-groups]; niagara keeps up to 8 levels at 0.6x, stopping when a level keeps 85% of the previous one's indices [ref:niagara-567ebe3-lod-chain]; Godot simplifies with meshoptimizer bottom-up, stopping at 75% of the source [ref:godot-4.4-lod-generation]. Levels sharing one vertex buffer cost only indices, under twice the source's for a halving chain; unique vertices per level about double vertex memory [ref:meshoptimizer-0.25-lod-chain]. Appearance-driven simplification fits geometry and material to rendered images and keeps a scene's look at 0.4% of its triangles, in hours per asset on a V100 [ref:hasselgren-2021-appearance-driven-simplification]; its code is non-commercial [ref:nvdiffmodeling-licence], so only the idea is usable.

**2. Selection by projected size.** meshoptimizer compares a level's error with one pixel's size at the distance to the bounding sphere [ref:meshoptimizer-0.25-lod-selection]; Godot's default threshold of one pixel is "perceptually lossless" [ref:godot-4.4-mesh-lod-threshold], Simplygon assumes errors under a pixel go unnoticed [ref:simplygon-10.4-on-screen-size], and Unreal and Unity switch on screen size, not distance [ref:ue-5.6-lod-screen-size] [ref:unity-6000.0-lodgroup-transition-height]. niagara picks the level per draw in its two-phase culling compute shader [ref:niagara-567ebe3-gpu-lod-select], the shape of Praxis's culling pass; Haar and Aaltonen's object culling with LOD took 0.28 ms on Xbox One at 1080p [ref:haar-aaltonen-benchmark].

**3. Hard switches and hysteresis.** Godot's instant mode uses hysteresis so a camera at the boundary does not flip levels back and forth [ref:godot-4.4-visibility-range-hysteresis]; Unreal's skeletal meshes delay only the switch to a simpler level [ref:ue-5.6-skeletal-lod-hysteresis]. Hoppe named the pop that instantaneous switching causes and proposed geomorphs against it [ref:hoppe-1996-progressive-meshes]; Nanite avoids both by keeping error under a pixel and letting TAA smooth the change [ref:nanite-taa-lod], judging geomorphs and cross-fades too expensive [ref:nanite-lod-no-geomorphing].

## Transitions without TAA

**4. Dithered cross-fade per pixel.** Both levels draw during the fade, weighted 1 to 0 and 0 to 1, usually through screen-space dithering [ref:unity-6000.0-lodgroup-transition-height]: Unity's URP offers a Bayer matrix, blue noise or a 2×2 stencil [ref:unity-urp-17-lod-crossfade-dither], and its core library keeps the pattern static because a moving one "confuses the TAA" [ref:unity-graphics-core-lod-dithering-taa]; Bevy uses a 4×4 ordered pattern in 16 steps [ref:bevy-0.20-visibility-range-dither]. Unreal's dithered transition is a material switch [ref:ue-5.8-dithered-lod-transition]; Cesium for Unreal built its own fade on an engine node "meant for temporal AA", found it "fails so badly with MSAA" under forward shading [ref:cesium-unreal-1104-dither-msaa], and fades over 0.5 s by default, both levels drawn [ref:cesium-2022-dithered-lod-transitions]. Godot says the noisy pattern "may not be as noticeable" with temporal antialiasing [ref:godot-4.5-dithered-lod]. No source publishes its GPU time.

**5. Cross-fade per sample.** With 4x MSAA, alpha-to-coverage gives five levels, but two surfaces at 50% write "the same two coverage samples" [ref:golus-2017-alpha-to-coverage], and the mask it derives from alpha is platform-dependent and may vary by pixel [ref:webgpu-alpha-to-coverage-mask]; NVIDIA exposes its dither as a switch [ref:nv-alpha-to-coverage-dither-control]. A fragment shader can instead write `sample_mask`, in Metal since version 1 [ref:msl-4.1-sample-mask-output]; WebGPU forbids that output together with alpha-to-coverage [ref:webgpu-sample-mask-vs-alpha-to-coverage], and wgpu's own field gives no pattern guarantee beyond alpha 0 and 1 [ref:wgpu-30.0.1-alpha-to-coverage]. Writing k of 4 samples for the outgoing level and the other 4 − k for the incoming one, varied per pixel over the standard sample positions [ref:webgpu-standard-sample-pattern], leaves no hole and no double cover wherever both levels cover the pixel; at silhouettes that differ, each level shows only its share, as a dither does. The resolve then averages the two levels within the pixel. In Praxis both depth passes and the depth-equal lit pass would write the same mask, which means adding it to `fs_surface_normals`, or a fragment stage to a pre-pass that has none, at a cost unmeasured. No shipped title or published cost was found for it.

**6. Geomorphing.** Hoppe's geomorph moves each fine vertex toward its ancestor in the coarser mesh, an ancestor map gathered as the mesh is refined [ref:hoppe-1996-geomorph]. CDLOD morphs terrain grids in the vertex shader with no seams, between two levels per node [ref:strugar-2010-cdlod-morph], and SpeedTree stores each vertex's next-level position [ref:unity-6000-speedtree-geomorph]. meshoptimizer 0.25's levels index the original vertices and give no ancestor map [ref:meshoptimizer-0.25-simplify-original-vertices], so the cooker would have to recover one.

## The far field

**7. HLOD per cell.** Erikson's HLODs replace groups of objects with merged simplifications [ref:erikson-2001-hlod]. Unreal's World Partition draws unloaded cells by HLOD layers with their own cell size and loading range [ref:ue-5.6-world-partition-hlod]; its layer types are instancing, merged, simplified and approximated (voxel) meshes [ref:ue-5.8-hlod-layer-types], the layer's own defaults 256 m cells loaded within 512 m, settings that moved to the partition's settings in 5.7 [ref:ue-5.8-hlod-layer-defaults]; 256 m is the size of Praxis's Chunks. The HLOD shows exactly when its cell does not, a hard toggle; with Nanite or virtual texturing on, the cell's unload waits five frames by default so the HLOD's assets show at the right resolution [ref:ue-5.8-hlod-swap-warmup]. Unity's HLOD sample cuts 5,642 draws to 952 and 8.0 M triangles to 3.9 M, hardware not stated [ref:unity-hlodsystem-04be7e8-readme]. Horizon Zero Dawn ships collapsed-geometry LODs under a parent and child LOD test with fade states [ref:decima-2017-collapsed-lods] and keeps its outermost ring of low-resolution tiles always loaded [ref:fahner-2023-hzd-tile-rings]; Far Cry 5 keeps the 2 km root tiles of its 10 × 10 km terrain resident [ref:moore-2018-fc5-terrain-quadtree], and Avalanche keeps simple landmark models resident, and gives as an example billboard tree patches that fade to a coarse distant forest far off and to full models up close [ref:persson-2012-landmarks-distant-forest].

**8. Proxies by remeshing.** Simplygon remeshes for a target on-screen size, filling gaps under a pixel and removing interiors [ref:simplygon-10.1-remeshing]; Unreal's approximated layer voxelizes at 1 m by default [ref:ue-5.8-hlod-approximate-voxel]; Insomniac automated Spider-Man 2's distant-city LODs when its manual pipeline was no longer viable [ref:kircher-2024-spider-man-2-distant-lods]. meshoptimizer 0.25's sloppy simplifier merges disjoint but close parts, and its prune flag drops isolated ones [ref:meshoptimizer-0.25-simplify-sloppy]; voxel remeshing arrives in 1.3 and returns positions only [ref:meshoptimizer-1.3-voxel-remesh], a version bump.

**9. Octahedral impostors.** An octahedral or hemi-octahedral grid of captured views, each pixel blending the three nearest frames; Fortnite's main pines use 12×12 views and its smaller farm trees 8×8, the fewest it uses [ref:brucks-2018-octahedral-impostors]. Fortnite's proxy HLODs moved from simplified trees, whose popping was "quite noticeable", to impostors at 6.8 MB of textures a tree type, saving about 500,000 drawn triangles in a forest for about 600 draws over the island [ref:brucks-2018-impostor-hlod-cost]. The traditional billboard mode's Dither option hides edge-on cards with DitherTemporalAA, which Brucks calls "not an option for all platforms", and its depth offset makes TAA "look smeary" [ref:brucks-2018-billboard-dither-taa]. SpeedTree cross-fades its 3D tree into billboards through alpha-to-coverage [ref:speedtree-runtime-billboard-a2c], the hand-over row 5 describes. The baker plugin carries no licence [ref:brucks-impostorbaker-c75d365]. Parallax-corrected impostors claim temporal aliasing "heavily suppressed" against the mesh, in research only [ref:misiak-2021-xr-impostors].

**10. Billboard clouds.** Décoret et al. project a model onto a few textured, alpha-masked planes without connectivity, fill-rate bound: 0.068 to 0.22 ms for 31 to 552 planes at about 128 pixels; the paper names a GeForce 4 Ti4600 only for its simplification timings [ref:decoret-2003-billboard-clouds]. No shipped engine was found using them.

## Culling at millions of Instances

**11. Flat GPU culling.** Nanite culls every Instance of a view in a single dispatch [ref:karis-2021-instance-cull-single-dispatch]: 896,322 Instances before culling, 3,668 after, its CullRasterize lines sum to 2,237 µs, under the slide's 2.5 ms, only if InstanceCull's "108ms" is 108 µs, about 0.12 ns an Instance; its post pass culls 102,804 in 125 µs, 1.2 ns each; hardware not stated [ref:karis-2021-instance-cull-cost]. Haar and Aaltonen's 0.28 ms for 250,000 objects with LOD is about 1.1 ns an object on Xbox One [ref:haar-aaltonen-benchmark]. Praxis's 4.5 ns a slot is an upper bound that includes the appends, and is 4 to 40 times those; why is unmeasured. Epic's artists raised Instance counts as fast as triangle counts: "instances are the new triangles" [ref:karis-2021-instances-new-triangles].

**12. Cells on the CPU, Instances on the GPU.** Assassin's Creed Unity culls coarsely on the CPU before GPU instance culling [ref:haar-aaltonen-2015-coarse-cpu-cull]. Horizon Zero Dawn sorts Instances by filter bits, LOD range and Morton order into clusters of at least 4K [ref:decima-2017-cluster-sort-key], walks tiles and clusters on the CPU and dispatches one compute job per visible cluster [ref:decima-2017-cpu-hierarchy-gpu-jobs], 500K to 1.5M static Instances in about 1 to 2 ms on PS4, including CPU waits [ref:decima-2017-query-time]. Activision moved culling and LOD to the GPU when the CPU "quickly became the bottleneck", over AABB hierarchies of model collections [ref:activision-2021-collection-hierarchy]. Unity 6's GPU Resident Drawer culls flat on the CPU, in a Burst parallel-for job over every Instance, 32 to a batch [ref:unity-graphics-6000.0-cpu-instance-culling-job]. Overlapping top-level bounds cut culling efficiency [ref:meister-2021-bvh-star-two-level]. No source compares hierarchical with flat culling on one scene.

**13. Chained indirect dispatches.** A hierarchy traversed level by level drains the GPU at each level, and the CPU must issue the worst-case depth, so empty dispatches follow [ref:karis-2021-hierarchy-dependent-dispatches]; an empty `ExecuteIndirect` on Xbox's graphics pipe costs about 90 ns on average, spiking to 500 ns or worse on a cache miss [ref:wihlidal-2024-empty-execute-indirect]; what an empty compute dispatch costs under wgpu is unmeasured. A fixed two-level depth (Chunk, then Instance) has no worst case to cover; Praxis's indirect buffers already rest on the Capability floor [ref:wgpu-core-30-indirect-required].

**14. Persistent threads.** Nanite traverses its hierarchy from a work queue in one dispatch, saving 10 to 60% (typically about 25%), relying on scheduling that D3D and HLSL do not define [ref:karis-2021-persistent-threads-scheduling]; NVIDIA's sample turns its persistent kernel off on other vendors' hardware [ref:vk-lod-clusters-persistent-nvidia-only]. Vulkan and Metal "say almost nothing" about forward progress between workgroups [ref:sorensen-2021-progress-spec-gap], Apple supplied a test that breaks the occupancy-bound model on A12 and A14 [ref:sorensen-2021-apple-obe-violation], Metal promises progress only between SIMD-groups of one threadgroup [ref:msl-4.1-simdgroup-forward-progress], and a WebGPU contributor wrote in 2021 that the group is "very careful to avoid forward progress guarantees" [ref:levien-2021-gpuweb-no-progress-guarantee].

## Small and thin features

**15. Culling by projected size.** OpenSceneGraph culls bounds under 2 pixels by default [ref:osg-3.6.5-small-feature-culling]; Unreal maps object size to a cull distance and warns of popping [ref:ue-5.6-cull-distance-volumes]; Unity culls layers early [ref:unity-6000.0-layer-cull-distances] and its GPU Resident Drawer fades small meshes out over a range rather than dropping them [ref:unity-graphics-6000.0-small-mesh-culling]. Pixel-sized triangles shade each pixel more than eight times in a simulated pipeline [ref:fatahalian-2010-small-triangle-overshade]. No source was found on sub-pixel animals such as `town`'s pigeons.

**16. Widening with coverage fade.** Phone-wire AA clamps a wire's width to one pixel and fades its alpha by the width it lost, and suggests the same for railings and antennas [ref:persson-2012-phone-wire-aa]; the demo's code needs the author's approval for commercial use [ref:persson-2012-phone-wire-aa-licence]. TressFX widens hair strands by 0.71 pixels [ref:tressfx-4.1-strand-expand], and Frostbite widens strands to account for the pixel size, warning that MSAA alone costs overdraw [ref:frostbite-2019-strand-pixel-width]. Persson's demo blends; under 4x MSAA the faded coverage could go through alpha-to-coverage or a written `sample_mask` (row 5), so wires stay opaque and unsorted. Its width depends on the distance, so the vertex shader needs the wire's axis.

**17. Rays against LOD'd geometry.** Secondary rays must see the raster's LODs or shadows break [ref:lloyd-2020-stochastic-lod-dxr]; NVIDIA suggests the same LOD for both, or back-face culling where they differ [ref:nvidia-2022-rtx-best-practices-lod-culling]; Epic's fallback meshes self-intersected until a short back-face-culled ray preceded the long one [ref:wright-2022-lumen-fallback-self-intersection]; niagara builds each BLAS from the coarsest level whose error stays under a shadow threshold [ref:niagara-567ebe3-lod-chain]. Far proxies in the near field's TLAS cost Epic 44% of near-field traversal [ref:wright-2022-lumen-far-field-overlap], which a separate TLAS avoids [ref:netzel-costa-2022-rtow-far-field-tlas]; AC Shadows fills far geometry from a 128² cubemap [ref:leblanc-conte-2025-acs-far-cubemap]. Survey 4 holds the options.

## Options for M5 (no recommendation)

- **Levels.** G1: cooked chains from `meshopt` 0.6.2, each level halving, sharing the source's vertices; G2: G1 with unique vertices per level, about twice the vertex memory; G3: G1 plus voxel remeshing for proxies, a meshoptimizer 1.3 bump (an owner decision).
- **Selection.** S1: per Instance in the culling pass, one-pixel error at 1440p, with hysteresis; S2: S1 with a bias per Budget tier.
- **Transitions.**
  - T1: hard switch with hysteresis, judged on a Reference scene's gallery at 1440p.
  - T2: per-pixel dither over a set time, its pattern visible for the fade.
  - T3: per-sample cross-fade with complementary `sample_mask` writes in both depth passes, prototyped first; any cost is unpublished.
  - T4: geomorphs, an ancestor map recovered in the cooker.
- **Far field past the Load radius.**
  - F1: an HLOD proxy per Chunk (merged, simplified or sloppy), swapped hard against its Chunk.
  - F2: F1 with a cross-fade from T2 or T3.
  - F3: octahedral impostors for trees and clutter, atlases in the Budget split.
  - F4: rings of coarser proxies per group of Chunks out to 2 km.
- **Culling.**
  - C1: measure why the flat cull costs 4.5 ns a slot before adding a hierarchy.
  - C2: cull Chunks on the CPU and dispatch the surviving slot ranges.
  - C3: a fixed two-level GPU hierarchy of chained indirect dispatches.
  - C4: persistent-thread traversal, which needs a forward-progress guarantee Metal does not give.
- **Thin features.** W1: widen wires, railings and poles to a pixel with coverage fade; W2: cull or fade small Instances by projected size (pigeons), with W1's carrier; W3: both.
- **Rays.** Survey 4's L1 to L3; a hard LOD switch rebuilds the static TLAS under ADR 0020:20's triggers.

- What a `sample_mask` output in the depth pre-pass costs on Apple's tile-based GPU.

- The hardware behind Nanite's InstanceCull figure and its unit, read as microseconds from the pass total.
- Why Praxis's flat cull costs 4.5 ns a slot.

- Shipped far-field schemes of The Witcher 3, Red Dead Redemption 2 and Ghost of Tsushima; nothing beyond session overviews was found.
