# Pre-mortem: what the ADRs cost world and game design

Written 2026-10-13 for ticket #92 (parent #90). The question: for a Skyrim- or RDR-class single-player open world, which features do ADRs 0001 to 0022, the scale targets and the Budgets (ADR 0013, the [ladder decision](https://github.com/gariasf/praxis/issues/75#issuecomment-6141801746)) make hard, expensive or impossible, and is each cost worth what it buys?

Severity, for this class of game: *blocks* means a feature the class expects has no path inside the ADRs as written; *costly* means a path exists but costs a Budget share, authoring work or a visible artefact; *cosmetic* means the cost is small or standard practice already pays it. Every rating names the ADR clause it comes from. Costs marked "measured" come from the M4 Pro runs in `docs/research/global-illumination-and-lighting.md` (Measured on the M4 Pro: a procedural scene without materials, so lower bounds for shading); "computed" means arithmetic on the cited figures. Milestones are the ladder's: M3 main pass, M4 streamed world, M5 GI, M6 world content, M7 gameplay core, M9 editor.

## Blocks

**Skinned characters in a crowd.** ADR 0020 drops cascaded shadow maps and gives shadows only through one hard shadow ray per pixel per light; probe rays read the same TLAS. A skinned character casts a correct shadow only if its BLAS follows its pose each frame, and wgpu-core rebuilds every BLAS in full ("only rebuild is implemented", GI note, What wgpu 30 allows). A hundred 5,120-triangle BLAS take 7.8 to 9.3 ms of GPU to rebuild (measured, "the per-frame price of deforming meshes"), 65 to 78% of ADR 0013's 12 ms (computed), before a settlement's other work. What the constraint buys: one shadow path with no shadow-map aliasing and bounce from moving geometry. Escape hatches: rigid per-bone proxies (capsules or bone-segment meshes) as Instances in the dynamic TLAS, inside its 10k cap but with proxy-shaped shadows; or BLAS refit through wgpu-hal, which ADR 0007 permits for a subsystem past wgpu's safe API and which Metal's hal implements (GI note), at the cost of per-backend code and the BLAS bookkeeping problem Bevy hit on Metal. Neither is measured. Lights are costly, not blocked: every shadowed light adds a shadow ray per lit pixel in its Froxel (ADR 0019's lit pass, ADR 0020). One sun ray from every visible pixel costs 1.8 to 2.0 ms of GPU in the open scene and 3.7 to 3.9 ms in the dense one (measured); short local-light rays are cheaper, unmeasured. A night street of torches multiplies that. Escape: a cast-shadow flag per light and a cap on shadowed lights per Froxel, authored per settlement.

**Volumetric clouds and fog.** ADR 0008 and ADR 0020 leave no screen-space history anywhere (class (c) "is used nowhere"). Shipped volumetric clouds rely on it: Horizon Zero Dawn's clouds cost "around 20 milliseconds" until they updated "1 out of 16 pixels for each 4x4 pixel block" of a quarter-resolution buffer per frame and reprojected the previous frame, bringing the target to about 2 ms on a PlayStation 4 ([HZD p93](https://www.advances.realtimerendering.com/s2015/The%20Real-time%20Volumetric%20Cloudscapes%20of%20Horizon%20-%20Zero%20Dawn%20-%20ARTR.pdf#page=93), [p94](https://www.advances.realtimerendering.com/s2015/The%20Real-time%20Volumetric%20Cloudscapes%20of%20Horizon%20-%20Zero%20Dawn%20-%20ARTR.pdf#page=94), [p95](https://www.advances.realtimerendering.com/s2015/The%20Real-time%20Volumetric%20Cloudscapes%20of%20Horizon%20-%20Zero%20Dawn%20-%20ARTR.pdf#page=95), [p96](https://www.advances.realtimerendering.com/s2015/The%20Real-time%20Volumetric%20Cloudscapes%20of%20Horizon%20-%20Zero%20Dawn%20-%20ARTR.pdf#page=96) [ref:schneider-2015-hzd-clouds]), which is class (c). Without it the same march costs the full price every frame or shows its noise. What the constraint buys: no ghosting or smear behind moving objects against the sky. Escape hatch: a class (b) cache in world or sky space (clouds rendered into an amortised sky-space volume, fog accumulated in a camera-centred world-space volume), which the Temporal class definition in `CONTEXT.md` names for lighting terms but no survey has checked for participating media. Hair and fur are costly, not blocked: cards with alpha-to-coverage (ADR 0019) are the shipped answer for this class; strand hair is the part that needs history. Particles lose temporal upsampling but keep soft depth fades, which read the same frame's depth.

**Co-op.** ADR 0010 puts netcode out of scope, and "reopening any of these is a new effort, not a feature"; ADR 0022's determinism holds for the same binary on the same machine only, so lockstep across platforms is off the table too. What it buys: no replication, prediction or authority constraints in any system. The escape hatch costs nothing now: ADRs 0006 and 0022 already keep game state as serialisable data advanced by per-Tick inputs, the shape a later netcode effort would start from.

## Costly

**Foliage.** Alpha-to-coverage at 4x MSAA has five coverage levels per pixel, so soft alpha edges band without a temporal resolve (ADR 0008), and alpha-tested mips lose coverage with distance unless the cooker preserves it, a cook-time fix of the kind ADR 0018 already applies to roughness (Toksvig). Forward shading pays quad overdraw on thin geometry: Activision saw forward foliage at quad occupancy as low as 20% (visibility note, technique 1). Alpha-tested geometry in ray queries runs the candidate-and-confirm loop with a texture fetch per candidate (GI note, What wgpu 30 allows), unmeasured. Wind sway in the vertex shader leaves the BLAS still, so shadows do not sway and leaves self-shadow at their edges. Escape: grass stays out of the TLAS and receives shadows only, with the class (a) contact term ADR 0020 already allows; trees sway in shadow only through per-frame BLAS updates, priced as in the crowd paragraph.

**LOD at 2 km.** ADR 0019 rejects virtual geometry because Nanite hides its LOD switches with TAA and wgpu 30 cannot build an acceleration structure that follows the cut; dense static meshes use discrete LOD, which pops without history. Escape: a dithered cross-fade through alpha-to-coverage (per frame, class (a), but five levels at 4x), geomorphing between authored LODs, and HLOD proxies for the far field, which M4 already carries. Cost: authoring each LOD chain and a visible transition band.

**Time of day and overcast.** ADR 0020 keeps hard sun shadows: soft ones need about 4 rays per pixel, about 7 ms on the M4 Pro's open scene, or a class (c) denoiser. The sun's 0.53° gives about 0.9 cm of penumbra per metre of occluder distance (ADR 0020), so a tree 20 m from its shadow should blur over about 18 cm (computed) and here does not. Under overcast skies the sun fades and light comes from smooth probes, which carry no contact darkening (ADR 0020, Consequences). Escape: ADR 0020's own reopen triggers (a Golden showing hard sun edges at distance, or floating objects) and a class (a) contact or sky-occlusion term.

**Interiors and caves.** Walls thinner than the probe spacing leak, so every house and cave needs an authored Interior volume (ADR 0020); caves have no clean walls to fit one to. Interiors entered without a load must be resident with their Chunk, inside the fixed arenas (ADR 0017). Escape: per-interior probe sets, which ADR 0020 defers "until a Reference scene shows the masks failing". Cost: authoring per space, and arena room for interiors nobody enters.

**Water and transparency.** Transparency is a per-Instance sorted forward pass (ADR 0019): intersecting smoke, glass and water sort wrongly until order-independent transparency arrives. No screen-space history rules out screen-space reflections that accumulate; mirror reflections are one ray per pixel (ADR 0020), and one primary ray per pixel to 2 km costs 4.1 to 5.7 ms of GPU in the open scene before shading the hit (measured), which water covering half the screen roughly halves (computed). Refraction reads the same frame's opaque colour, which is not history.

**Fast traversal.** A 2 km load radius holds about 192 Chunks of 256 m (computed); moving at 50 m/s brings about 3 new Chunks per second into it (computed, 15.6 Chunks per 256 m of leading edge). Fast travel replaces the whole radius at once. The arenas never grow and a load that does not fit is refused (ADR 0017), so each Chunk's geometry must fit a Budget the cooker enforces: one vertex arena of at most 2^31 − 1 bytes holds about 76.7M of ADR 0018's 28-byte vertices, one index arena about 179M triangles (computed). Disk bandwidth per Chunk is not yet known. Escape: HLOD for the outer radius, a cooker that rejects over-Budget Chunks, and a fade or hold on fast travel.

**Destruction and many moving objects.** The dynamic TLAS is capped at 10k Instances (0.64 to 0.92 ms of CPU) and a 100k rebuild costs 6 to 8 ms of CPU (ADR 0020); a moving Instance past the cap casts no shadow and leaves no bounce. Fracture that makes new geometry at runtime needs arena room, new BLAS builds (0.6 to 0.75 ms of GPU for one 5,120-triangle BLAS, measured) and bypasses cooking (ADR 0018). Escape: pre-fractured cooked pieces, which enter the dynamic TLAS as they move.

**Player building.** The runtime loads cooked Blobs by Asset ID only (ADR 0018) and the static TLAS is rebuilt only when a Chunk streams or the anchor moves (ADR 0020). Building from cooked pieces works as entities placed at runtime, saved as data; each placed piece either joins the dynamic TLAS, inside its cap, or forces a static rebuild off the frame. Freeform generated meshes need an in-memory cook path, an amendment to ADR 0018.

**Modding.** Mods ship sources the runtime cannot read; the cooker sits behind a `cook` feature a shipping build turns off (ADR 0018), so modders need it as a separate tool, and override by mapping a vanilla Asset ID to their Blob in a cook index. Lua reacts to events and runs coroutines but never authors per-frame systems (ADR 0012), so mods that rewrite engine behaviour each frame need engine code. Cost: shipping and supporting the cooker; a mod API drawn at the event boundary.

**Cutscenes and camera cuts.** Probe cascades are camera-centred and converge to 90% within 0.5 s (ADR 0020), about 30 frames at 60 fps (computed), so a cut to a new place shows lagging indirect light. Cutscenes know their next camera, so cascades can pre-warm around it inside the 2 ms probe Budget; a cut can also drop DDGI's hysteresis for its first frames, still class (b).

**Save compatibility.** An Asset ID hashes the glTF name, so a mesh renamed in Blender gets a new ID and every save referencing the old one breaks; the cooker only warns (ADR 0018). Escape: an alias table from old to new IDs in the manifest.

**Reach below the Capability floor.** ADR 0021 has no fallback for ray queries: Vulkan devices without `VK_KHR_ray_query` are 52% of gpuinfo reports from the last year (ADR 0021), and macOS before 15 and DX12 below raytracing Tier 1.1 or SM 6.5 are out. ADR 0008 accepts this ("cutting-edge hardware, not mass-market scalability"). A raster fallback would be a second renderer.

**8 GB of VRAM.** ADR 0013 caps GPU memory at 8 GB counted as the engine's own buffers and textures, but an 8 GB card shares its memory with the OS and compositor, and D3D12 over budget can be "intermittently frozen" (streaming note, technique 8). The texture pool degrades to blur under a smaller Budget (ADR 0017); the geometry arenas do not, since a load that does not fit is refused. Escape: size Budgets at device creation from the adapter's reported budget (D3D12 `QueryVideoMemoryInfo`, Vulkan's memory-budget extension), with geometry the floor below which a Chunk cannot load.

## Options

- **A. Accept the costs as rated.** The three *blocks* stay open until their milestone: crowds at M3 or M7, volumetrics at M6, co-op never. Each costly row's escape is decided when its milestone opens.
- **B. Measure the two technical blocks early.** Add a crowd measurement (100 deforming BLAS, refit through wgpu-hal against rigid proxies) to M3's provisional criteria, and a "State of the art: volumetrics without screen-space history" survey to M6's boundary that also asks whether the Temporal class covers participating media.
- **C. Amend ADRs now.** ADR 0020 gains a raster shadow fallback for skinned characters, or ADR 0008 admits class (c) for volumetric media only. Either reverses a decision before a measurement asks for it.
