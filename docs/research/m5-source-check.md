# Source check: survey 3 and survey 4's M5 half

Checked 2026-11-13 for ticket #792 (map #790), against `main` at `d4ac21eb`: `docs/research/lod-hlod-and-far-field.md` (survey 3) in full, and `docs/research/acceleration-structures-for-streamed-worlds.md` (survey 4) rows 11 to 14 and "Options for M5". ## Summary

Of the 94 registry entries opened, 89 say what the surveys claim, three are wrong and two are unsupported. No entry is stale or dead, and the un-keyed claims also hold: Praxis's own figures, every licence cell, and the `meshopt` 0.6.2 crate, which vendors meshoptimizer 0.25 exactly. Four corrections change the evidence under an M5 option, though none removes one: S1 loses its shipped precedent (the 0.28 ms benchmark is RedLynx's test scene, not AC Unity), F4 loses Horizon as a ring precedent, W1's Frostbite cost and maturity narrow, and M1's support drops to two of the three vendors cited, since AMD splits overlapping instances rather than merging them.

## Method

- **Scope.** Survey 3 cites 96 distinct keys and survey 4's M5 half 22 more, 118 in all. The ticket's load-bearing list was taken in full: every published cost in the comparison tables, the transition claims behind T1 to T4, the forward-progress claims behind C4, meshoptimizer's claims behind G1 to G3, every licence claim, and every key under survey 4's rows 11 to 14 and its M5 options. That is 88 keys.
- **Sample.** The other 30 keys in document order, every fifth: `hasselgren-2021-appearance-driven-simplification`, `hoppe-1996-progressive-meshes`, `nv-alpha-to-coverage-dither-control`, `ue-5.8-hlod-approximate-voxel`, `karis-2021-instances-new-triangles`, `fatahalian-2010-small-triangle-overshade`. - **How each source was opened.** GitHub sources as raw files at the pinned commit or tag (Unreal Engine 5.8.4 through `gh api`, wgpu also against the crate source under `~/.cargo/registry`); PDFs downloaded and read at the cited page with `pdftotext`, a page rendered where its text is an image; `.pptx` decks unzipped and each notes file matched to its slide through the relationships; web pages at the registry's url, live or archived as registered. GitHub issues and pull requests through `gh`. Nothing was vouched from memory; no source failed to open (Décoret et al.'s HAL url answered a bot check, and its web.archive.org copy was read).
- **Verdicts** follow #90's rubric: the survey's sentence or table cell against what the source says, numbers, units, hardware and scope included.
