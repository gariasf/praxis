# World content: sources, licences, fit

Researched 2026-11-21 for ticket #947 (parent #944), against each publisher's own pages. It extends `docs/research/test-assets.md` from test models to the content a world is built from: terrain, vegetation, rocks and ground, water, vehicles, and the tools that generate them. Licences are quoted as the publisher states them, in `docs/references.toml`. This is engineering due diligence, not legal advice. Nothing was downloaded, chosen or added to the manifest: only licence and product pages were read, and two records of Poly Haven's API.

**Summary.** The licences let a public MIT repo hold or fetch real terrain and CC0 rocks, ground and plants; trees built for real time, grass and vehicles are the gaps.

- **Terrain is covered.** Public elevation data may be redistributed, for a credit at most: Spain at 2 m and 5 m under CC BY 4.0, the United States at 1 m to 10 m in the public domain, Switzerland on a grid down to 0.5 m under terms that make a source line mandatory. It arrives as GeoTIFF or ESRI ASCII rasters, which ADR 0011 does not name as a source format.
- **Rocks, ground and plants are covered by CC0 scans.** Poly Haven lists 110 nature models and ships glTF; ambientCG adds CC0 surfaces.
- **Trees exist under CC0, most of them too heavy for real time as shipped.** Poly Haven's 20 run from a 33,706-polygon dead trunk to a pine of 17.4 million polygons in a 949 MB buffer, with a median near 750,000. The trees made with the modeller games use (SpeedTree's ORCA pack) are noncommercial: dev-only.
- **Generators split in three.** Blender states that its output belongs to its author. EZ-Tree (MIT) and Infinigen (BSD) say nothing of their output. Every free edition of a commercial terrain tool read here (Gaea, World Machine, Houdini) limits its output to noncommercial use, and The Grove forbids distributing its trees at all.
- **Vehicles are the widest gap.** Those found are a toy car, a concept car and a milk truck carrying trademark notices, and two cars and a bicycle as OBJ files with no rig stated.
- **A pinned URL that needs no login** was found for two sources only: Poly Haven, whose API lists direct file URLs (none was requested), and Copernicus' elevation model on AWS, whose registry page says no account is needed. For the rest the licence allows a fetch and the download path was not read.
- **Of the rows `test-assets.md` left unverified,** four are resolved (the McGuire archive, Sketchfab, Blender's demo files, glTF-Validator), as is MDT02's licence. Fab's agreement is resolved and makes its content dev-only, but no Megascans listing was read. Intel still names no version of its Creative Commons licence.

The note feeds one line of #944's *Not yet specified*, the Reference scene for world content, and ends in options for it. It decides nothing.

## Classes

As `docs/research/test-assets.md` and ADR 0025 set them: **committed** (a small file in `assets/`, with a manifest entry and a credit), **fetched** (by `praxis fetch` from a pinned URL with its hash, never committed) and **dev-only** (never committed, never fetched in CI, and no committed Golden rendered from it). The table says *open* where the quoted licence allows redistribution and commercial use, so a file is committed or fetched by its size; *owner's call* where it allows them under conditions MIT does not state, or says nothing; and dev-only for a noncommercial term, a bar on redistribution or automation, or a licence the page does not confirm.

## Quality

The surveys of #944 had not landed when this was written, so quality is given in the terms the ticket names, from what the publishers state.

- **Scan resolution.** Poly Haven's nature models carry Textures of 4096², 8192² or 16384², its trees 8192². ambientCG's and Fab's were not read.
- **LOD chains.** No open source states one built for real time. Poly Haven sets a `lods` flag on 72 of 110 nature models and does not say how many levels it means. Its 20 trees span 33,706 to 17,427,094 polygons with a median of 746,644.5; the category also holds stumps, trunks and saplings, and a standing tree such as `jacaranda_tree` is 312,356. `pine_tree_01`, the heaviest, has a 948,849,556-byte glTF buffer [ref:polyhaven-api-pine-tree-files]: the cooker would have to simplify such a model into a chain before any budget holds. EZ-Tree generates its levels from one skeleton, with fewer and larger leaves further out. SpeedTree exports LODs and atlases; the Sponza cypresses and EZ-Tree use leaf cards.
- **Leaves and bark as separate Materials.** Poly Haven's pine keeps bark, trunk and twig Texture sets apart. EZ-Tree has one bark and one leaf Material. The others were not read to this depth, since that needs the files.
- **Texel density.** Poly Haven publishes a figure per model (`texel_density`), without its unit; across the 20 trees it spans 392 to 230,007, so the library is not uniform and a scene would have to normalise it.

## Tiers and dates

- **Fab.** Three tiers. Personal and Professional "grant you the same scope of rights" and differ in who may buy them: Personal only under 100,000 USD of revenue from "commercial activity in the digital content industry" in the last twelve months, funds raised included. The third, Reference-Only, gives a referenced asset and no source file [ref:fab-eula-tiers]. By date: content "acquired (whether free or paid) prior to the modified terms will remain governed by the license terms applicable at the time" [ref:fab-eula-amendments], so content acquired under earlier terms, Quixel's before Fab among them, keeps those terms, which were not read here.
- **Gaea.** Editions are tied to "annual gross revenue or capital" (Indie to 100,000 USD, Professional to 1,000,000) [ref:gaea-eula], and a licence update binds only versions released after it: an installed version keeps the agreement it shipped with [ref:gaea-eula-versions].
- **Sketchfab.** Two clauses pull apart. A download is taken under "the license that applies at the time of download", yet use of it "will always be subject to the most-current version" of that licence [ref:sketchfab-terms-free-downloads]. The record to keep is the licence the page shows on the day of download.

## The rows `test-assets.md` left unverified

- **McGuire Computer Graphics Archive**: resolved. The page frames `g3d/data10/index.html`, whose models each carry a licence in an `info.js` record; six were read and are in the table. The licences are the archive's statements about work it converted.
- **Fab and Megascans**: Fab's agreement is resolved, and content under its Standard License is dev-only. That Megascans listings sit under it and under no terms of their own, and whether they are free of charge, were not confirmed: Quixel's page rendered empty on the date read and no listing was opened.
- **Sketchfab**: resolved as a per-model licence chosen by the uploader, behind a login, under site terms that restrict republishing.
- **Blender demo files**: resolved as per-file CC0, CC-BY or CC-BY-SA where stated, with versions mostly unstated.
- **glTF-Validator**: Apache-2.0 [ref:gltf-validator-licence]: usable as a tool or dependency under the legal filter of #944.
- **MDT02**: CC BY 4.0 for its second coverage, as MDT05's first.
- **Intel's licence version**: still unstated on 2026-11-15's snapshot of the page. Stays marked.

## Options for a world-content Reference scene

Each uses only sources whose licence lets CI fetch them or the repo hold them; the download path of each is confirmed at need. They combine: A is the floor under any of the others.

- **A. Procedural, in the repo.** A heightfield from noise and erosion written here, trees from a generator written here. No licence, deterministic, tiny (Tier 1 of `test-assets.md`). It exercises every system and shows nothing of how scanned content behaves.
- **B. Real terrain, CC0 scans.** A crop of public elevation (USGS 3DEP in the public domain, or MDT02 under CC BY 4.0 for a credit), dressed with Poly Haven rocks, ground cover, plants and ground Materials. All fetched. It needs a heightfield import that does not exist, and the fetch is large if it takes the heavy trees: the pine alone is about 1 GB. The Poly Haven URL read carries no version, so a re-upload would fail the hash until the manifest moves.
- **C. Real terrain, authored trees.** B's terrain and rocks, with trees authored once in Blender, whose output is its author's, and kept as small glTF files, committed or fetched from the repo's own hosting. The LOD chain, leaf cards and Material split are then under the engine's control. The trees look modelled, not scanned. EZ-Tree would shorten the authoring if the owner takes its silence on output as leave.
- **D. A dev-only realism set beside any of them.** Fab content or the ORCA SpeedTree pack and Emerald Square on the owner's machine, for the visual audit and local benches. No Golden, no CI, no file in the repo.

B and C differ in where the trees come from; the terrain survey decides whether real elevation or a generated heightfield is the better test.

## Gaps

- **Vehicles.** No source read offers a photoreal vehicle as glTF under CC0 or CC BY that is known to be built for driving: wheels as separate nodes, a collision Mesh. `ToyCar` is a toy; `CarConcept` and `CesiumMilkTruck` carry trademark notices; the McGuire Sports Car, BMW and Road Bike are OBJ files with no rig stated, the BMW "a single car with wheels in a default pose". Whether any of them separates its wheels was not checked, since that needs the file.
- **Trees for real time.** CC0 trees exist; the full-grown ones are heavy, and none states a chain down to cards or an impostor. Wind data comes from the generators (EZ-Tree animates it, Tree It exports it as vertex colours), not from the scans. Trees made the way games make them are noncommercial (ORCA) or sold under terms not read (SpeedTree).
- **Grass.** Poly Haven lists four grass models, and Blender's CC0 "Tree, leaves and grass" file is a node setup. No CC0 library of grass cards or atlases was confirmed; ambientCG's Atlas type was not read.
- **Water.** A water surface needs no asset. No source was read for flow maps, foam Textures or shoreline data, and the one bathymetry source read is GEBCO's 15 arc-second global grid.
- **Terrain as a source format.** The elevation sources read deliver GeoTIFF or ESRI ASCII grids, and ADR 0011 names glTF. What a heightfield is authored and cooked from is a question for the terrain survey.
- **Terrain colour.** Orthophotos and land-cover data, which a terrain's Materials would be painted from, were not surveyed.

## Not verified

- That Megascans sits under the Fab Standard License, its price, and the terms of content acquired before the agreement read.
- SpeedTree's terms for redistributing an exported model. EZ-Tree and Infinigen state nothing about their output; Tree It states only that it may be sold.
- Intel's Creative Commons version, and the version behind each bare "CC-BY" on Blender's demo page.
- The original authors' licences behind the McGuire archive's records.
- A login-free URL to pin for USGS, CNIG, swisstopo, NASA, ambientCG and Smithsonian; that the AWS copy of Copernicus' model carries the licence text read; the general terms of Switzerland's federal geodata infrastructure.
- Whether Sketchfab's terms let a CC0 or CC BY model be re-hosted, and the licence of any single Sketchfab or Fab listing.
- What ambientCG's Terrain and Atlas types and Smithsonian's collection hold.
- What Poly Haven's `lods` flag and `texel_density` unit mean, whether its glTF packages carry the LODs, and the formats of any model but the one read.
- blender.org, intel.com and si.edu refused a direct fetch; their pages were read from web.archive.org snapshots of 2026-11-15 to 2026-11-18.
