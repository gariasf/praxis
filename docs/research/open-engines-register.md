# Open engines worth reading: the register

Researched 2026-11-21 for ticket #946 (parent #944). The register of engines and repositories worth reading for world systems (terrain, vegetation and grass, water and oceans, rain, snow, wetness and wind, simulated vehicles), each at a named commit, with its licence class under #944's legal filter, its fit to ADRs 0008, 0019 and 0020, and the path of each system it holds. It is read engine by engine, where the five surveys it unblocks (#948 to #952) are read system by system. It judges no technique and no cost.

**Summary.** Thirty-six entries are kept and three dropped; the five surveys start from the reading lists at the end, and nothing here asks a decision of the owner. No entry matches the stack on every count: each is evidence for the surveys, never a template.

- **Wicked Engine** (MIT) holds all five systems in a Forward+ renderer with MSAA, on DirectX 12, Vulkan and Metal, with ray queries for its probes: chunked terrain with a virtual texture, grass that has an acceleration structure of its own, an FFT ocean, rain kept out from under cover by a depth map drawn from above, a per-vertex wet map, a wind volume, and Jolt's vehicles. Spartan Engine and Torque3D also fill all five columns of the table, each with a deferred path.
- **Spartan Engine is MIT only up to commit `cb258a1` (2026-09-24).** The next commit replaced MIT with a source-available licence, noncommercial at first and royalty-bearing as it reads now, so later commits are evidence only and their source was not opened. The car the owner named as the bar for #952 is in the MIT commit: a Pacejka tyre, a suspension and driveline built from PhysX bodies and joints, anti-roll bars, a 200 Hz fixed step.
- **Godot, Bevy and O3DE.** Godot 4.7.2's Forward+ is clustered forward with MSAA and has TAA off by default, and holds no terrain, water or grass of its own. Bevy 0.20.1 shares wgpu 30 and holds, of the five, one example water material; the shaders nearest the stack are in its ecosystem: WGSL in `bevy_terrain` (Bevy 0.14) and `bevy_feronia` (Bevy 0.18), WESL in `bevy_hanabi`. O3DE's Forward+ pipeline defaults to MSAA and holds terrain, with meshes of its own for ray tracing, and placement for vegetation.
- **Vehicles.** Jolt (MIT) and PhysX 5.13 (Apache-2.0) model an engine torque curve, clutch, gearbox, differentials, anti-roll bars and tyre friction curves; Rapier, Bullet and Godot hold a ray-cast vehicle with no driveline. A Pacejka tyre is in Spartan's car, in a PhysX sample, in Chrono (BSD-3-Clause, a multi-physics simulation package) and in VDrift (GPL-3.0-or-later, evidence only).
- **Water.** Five of the seven FFT oceans read give the CPU a wave height by reading results back from the GPU: Wicked and Spartan the displacement map, Crest the answers to query points, GarrettGunnell/Water its first band, web-ocean one height under the camera. No CPU read was found in the other two (GodotOceanWaves, Proland). Crest can also bake the shape, and Unity's documentation offers a CPU copy of the simulation as a mode it advises against. That is the choice #950 has to hold against ADR 0022.
- **Rain under cover** has three forms in what was read: a depth map from above (Wicked), a height field the particles collide with, which can follow the camera (Godot), a ray per drop (Torque3D).
- **Licences inside permissive trees.** `godot_voxel`'s Transvoxel tables, O3DE's probe GI shaders, Bullet's ray-cast vehicle, Crest's planar reflection and example assets, and `bevy_feronia`'s example assets are under other terms than their repositories. PhysX's SDK and Rapier are Apache-2.0 alone.
- **Beyond the five systems**, eleven kinds of system are on no line of the ladder's map; none binds a decision that the surveys or the pre-mortem do not already carry, so the register proposes lines for the map and no ticket.

Sources: 36 repositories read at the commits named below, through shallow clones that were never checked out, built or run; the public documentation of Unreal Engine 5.9, Unity 6.6 with HDRP 17.0 and Flax, with no source of those three opened beyond Flax's licence file. Each licence and each claim relied on is in `docs/references.toml`, cited by key; every quote there was matched by script against the file at its commit, or against a saved copy of its page, on 2026-11-21. Every path named was opened at its entry's commit. Nothing was measured.

Licence classes, from #944's Notes: **A**, adaptable with citation; **E**, evidence only, nothing copied or adapted; **P**, Apache-2.0 alone, usable as a dependency and read as source, adapting it a case for the owner.

## Engines

**Wicked Engine** v0.72.139 (`fc8385b`), MIT [ref:wicked-engine-fc8385b-licence], source read. Fit: DirectX 12, Vulkan and Metal with HLSL; Forward+ with MSAA; TAA is one listed option; probes traced by inline ray queries, and ray-traced ambient occlusion, shadows and reflections on DXR and Vulkan [ref:wicked-engine-fc8385b-features] [ref:wicked-engine-fc8385b-world-systems] [ref:wicked-engine-fc8385b-ddgi-ray-queries]. The feature list also holds tessellation and stochastic alpha-test transparency, and does not say which features need TAA. Terrain (`WickedEngine/wiTerrain.h`, `WickedEngine/wiTerrain.cpp`): chunks of 64 cells made around the camera, an index buffer per LOD over one vertex grid with an apron, a virtual texture with a feedback map, grass and props per chunk [ref:wicked-engine-fc8385b-terrain]. Grass (`WickedEngine/wiHairParticle.h`, `WickedEngine/shaders/hairparticle_simulateCS.hlsl`): strands of segments drawn as billboards, simulated in compute, with an acceleration structure of their own [ref:wicked-engine-fc8385b-grass]. Ocean (`WickedEngine/wiOcean.cpp`, `WickedEngine/shaders/oceanSimulatorCS.hlsl`): a Phillips spectrum, advanced in compute by the shader named, and an FFT; the CPU's height query is served from a read-back of the displacement map, and buoyancy calls it from `WickedEngine/wiPhysics_Jolt.cpp` [ref:wicked-engine-fc8385b-ocean-readback]. Weather (`WickedEngine/wiScene_Components.h`, `WickedEngine/wiScene.cpp`, `WickedEngine/wiRenderer.cpp`, `WickedEngine/shaders/wetmap_updateCS.hlsl`, `WickedEngine/shaders/windCS.hlsl`): one weather component holds wind, rain and the ocean's parameters; rain is a GPU particle emitter that reads a rain blocker, a depth map drawn from above into the shadow atlas; a per-vertex wet map dries by slope and reads the same blocker and the ocean's height; wind is a 3D texture of noise, written each frame beside the previous frame's value [ref:wicked-engine-fc8385b-rain-blocker] [ref:wicked-engine-fc8385b-wind]. The weather component has no snow field. Vehicles are Jolt's wheeled and motorcycle controllers [ref:wicked-engine-fc8385b-vehicle]. Maturity: its readme lists sample games and a game creator [ref:wicked-engine-fc8385b-readme].

**Spartan Engine** at `cb258a1` (2026-09-24), MIT at that commit [ref:spartan-engine-cb258a1-licence], source read there and nowhere later. Commit `230a887` replaced MIT with the Spartan Engine License 1.0, source-available and noncommercial by its own text; at the head commit (`5f3f9f2`, 2026-11-19) it is version 2.0, which calls itself source-available, grants noncommercial use, takes a royalty on commercial products, and leaves earlier MIT copies under MIT [ref:spartan-engine-5f3f9f2-licence]; of the commits after `cb258a1` only licence files were read, and its file list shows weather, rain and terrain deformation added since. Fit: Vulkan and DirectX 12 with HLSL; deferred; its readme lists TAA with upsampling, DLSS, XeSS and FXAA and no MSAA; ReSTIR path tracing, a froxel fog with temporal reprojection, and ray-traced reflections and shadows through ray queries [ref:spartan-engine-cb258a1-rendering]. Car (`source/car/CarSimulation.h`, `source/car/CarPacejka.h`, `source/car/CarMultibody.h`, `source/car/CarKinematics.h`, `source/car/CarThermal.h`, `source/car/CarTireDeformation.h`): a PhysX chassis body with per-wheel tyre, suspension, drivetrain and aerodynamics; Pacejka curves with load, temperature and surface factors; suspension links, anti-roll bars and the driveline as PhysX bodies and joints; a tyre thermal model [ref:spartan-engine-cb258a1-car] [ref:spartan-engine-cb258a1-car-source] [ref:spartan-engine-cb258a1-car-tyre] [ref:spartan-engine-cb258a1-car-multibody]. Physics steps at a fixed 200 Hz and its origin follows the camera (`source/physics/PhysicsWorld.cpp`, `source/physics/PhysicsSceneOrigin.h`) [ref:spartan-engine-cb258a1-physics-step]. Terrain (`source/world/components/Terrain.h`, `source/world/components/Terrain.cpp`, `data/shaders/common_terrain.hlsl`, `source/world/TerrainPlacement.h`): an eroded height field in tiles, procedural layers scored per pixel against a baked analysis and blended by height, scatter rules on the same analysis [ref:spartan-engine-cb258a1-terrain-surface]. Grass (`data/shaders/grass_populate.hlsl`, `data/shaders/grass_interaction.hlsl`): instances made and culled in compute each frame, and a persistent field of tyre tracks [ref:spartan-engine-cb258a1-grass] [ref:spartan-engine-cb258a1-grass-tracks]. Wind (`data/shaders/wind_field.hlsl`, `data/shaders/common_vertex_processing.hlsl`): one texture a frame, read by grass, flowers and trees [ref:spartan-engine-cb258a1-wind-field]. Ocean (`source/world/components/Water.h`, `source/world/components/Water.cpp`, `data/shaders/ocean/ocean_spectrum.hlsl`): FFT cascades on a camera-centred clipmap; buoyancy runs each fixed physics step on heights read back from the GPU (`source/world/components/Physics.cpp`, `source/rendering/Renderer.h`, `source/rendering/Renderer_Definitions.h`) [ref:spartan-engine-cb258a1-ocean-readback]. Else: GPU particles (`data/shaders/particles.hlsl`), skid marks (`source/world/components/SkidMarks.h`, `data/shaders/skid_marks.hlsl`). Maturity: by its readme a personal research engine, not a product [ref:spartan-engine-cb258a1-readme].

**Godot** 4.7.2-stable (`ed1daf0`), MIT [ref:godot-4.7.2-licence], source read. Fit: Metal, Vulkan and Direct3D 12 through its RenderingDevice, with GLSL; three rendering methods, and no deferred path in the renderer directories read [ref:godot-4.7.2-renderers]. Its Forward+ method is the nearest architecture to ADR 0019: a depth pre-pass, an opaque pass and MSAA resolves in `servers/rendering/renderer_rd/forward_clustered/render_forward_clustered.cpp`, with alpha to coverage as pipeline state in `servers/rendering/renderer_rd/forward_clustered/scene_shader_forward_clustered.cpp` [ref:godot-4.7.2-msaa-forward-clustered]. TAA is off by default and the documentation calls it incomplete [ref:godot-4.7.2-taa]; acceleration structures are an experimental device API that the renderer files read do not use [ref:godot-4.7.2-ray-tracing-api]. It holds no terrain renderer, water, grass or wind field. Weather: GPU particles collide with a real-time height field that can follow the camera (`scene/3d/gpu_particles_collision_3d.cpp`, `servers/rendering/renderer_rd/storage_rd/particles_storage.cpp`, `servers/rendering/renderer_rd/shaders/particles.glsl`), which its documentation offers for rain and snow and which cannot hold overhangs [ref:godot-4.7.2-particle-heightfield]; area wind acts on soft bodies only [ref:godot-4.7.2-wind-soft-body]. Vehicle (`scene/3d/physics/vehicle_body_3d.cpp`): a ray per wheel, a spring with two damping rates, a friction clamp, no gears; the documentation says it is not designed for realistic vehicle physics [ref:godot-4.7.2-vehicle]. Else: clustered decals (`scene/3d/decal.cpp`), soft bodies (`scene/3d/physics/soft_body_3d.cpp`), a character body (`scene/3d/physics/character_body_3d.cpp`), ragdolls (`scene/3d/physics/physical_bone_simulator_3d.cpp`), spring bones (`scene/3d/spring_bone_simulator_3d.cpp`).

**Terrain3D** v1.1.0-stable (`629bbd6`), MIT [ref:terrain3d-1.1.0-licence], source read; a C++ extension for Godot. Terrain (`src/terrain_3d_mesher.cpp`, `src/shaders/main.glsl`, `src/terrain_3d_data.h`, `src/terrain_3d_collision.cpp`): a geomorphing clipmap whose meshes are made once and recentred on the camera, heights read in the vertex shader, in regions [ref:terrain3d-1.1.0-clipmap] [ref:terrain3d-1.1.0-architecture]; an index control map blended at four grid points over texture arrays [ref:terrain3d-1.1.0-texturing]; holes by a control-map bit; a CPU height query and collision built around the camera (`doc/docs/collision.md`). Its notes say temporal effects (FSR, TAA, motion blur) make its textures flicker because the clipmap moves, and that Godot 4.5 with this release fixes it [ref:terrain3d-1.1.0-temporal]. Vegetation (`src/terrain_3d_instancer.cpp`, `src/terrain_3d_instancer.h`): instances in 32 m cells with a LOD per cell and a shadow impostor; the instancer provides no wind or interaction shader [ref:terrain3d-1.1.0-instancer]; an example places grass around the camera from the terrain's maps, with wind (`project/addons/terrain_3d/extras/particle_example/particles.gdshader`, `project/addons/terrain_3d/extras/particle_example/grass.gdshader`). Its extras hold an example ocean shader for the clipmap mesh (`project/addons/terrain_3d/extras/shaders/ocean_shader.gdshader`), and wetness is painted as roughness (`doc/docs/texture_painting.md`). Maturity: a stable release with a list of games (`doc/docs/games.md`).

**godot_voxel** v1.7 (`2ac9f5f`), MIT [ref:godot-voxel-1.7-licence], source read. Terrain as voxels meshed into chunks: blocky, or smooth from a distance field with Transvoxel, which allows overhangs and tunnels (`meshers/transvoxel/voxel_mesher_transvoxel.cpp`, `meshers/blocky/voxel_mesher_blocky.cpp`) [ref:godot-voxel-1.7-readme]; LOD as parented grids of blocks, each level twice the size of the one below, with triplanar texturing since a smooth surface has no UVs (`terrain/variable_lod/voxel_lod_terrain.h`, `doc/source/smooth_terrain.md`) [ref:godot-voxel-1.7-lod]. Its instancer scatters models on the surface by LOD (`terrain/instancing/voxel_instancer.cpp`, `doc/source/instancing.md`). Its blocky form has a fluid voxel model, named in `doc/source/blocky_terrain.md` and not read further. The Transvoxel tables in `meshers/transvoxel/transvoxel_tables.cpp` carry their own notice, which is not MIT and bars reproducing the file's contents without permission: that file is class E, and only its header was read [ref:godot-voxel-1.7-transvoxel-tables].

**HTerrain** (Zylann's heightmap plugin for Godot, `49887ff`), MIT [ref:hterrain-49887ff-licence], source read. Terrain (`addons/zylann.hterrain/util/quad_tree_lod.gd`, `addons/zylann.hterrain/hterrain_mesher.gd`, `addons/zylann.hterrain/hterrain_collider.gd`): a quadtree of chunks updated on the CPU, 16 seam variants of the chunk mesh, three texturing schemes described side by side in `addons/zylann.hterrain/doc/docs/index.md`, holes that are visual only [ref:hterrain-49887ff-docs]. Grass (`addons/zylann.hterrain/hterrain_detail_layer.gd`, `addons/zylann.hterrain/shaders/detail.gdshader`): instanced quads from a density map, alpha-tested, with an ambient sway. Its author no longer adds features [ref:hterrain-49887ff-maintenance].

**Bevy** 0.20.1 (`a0577b5`), MIT OR Apache-2.0 [ref:bevy-0.20.1-licence], source read. Fit: wgpu 30; every shader at this release is WESL, a superset of WGSL (212 `.wesl` files and no `.wgsl`); clustered forward and deferred; MSAA defaults to 4 samples [ref:bevy-0.20.1-msaa-default], and TAA, SSAO (`crates/bevy_pbr/src/ssao/mod.rs`), meshlets (`crates/bevy_pbr/src/meshlet/mod.rs`) and its ray-traced lighting each require it off [ref:bevy-0.20.1-taa-msaa] [ref:bevy-0.20.1-solari]. That lighting crate traces inline ray queries on wgpu 30 (`crates/bevy_solari/src/scene/bindings.wesl`) and needs a deferred prepass and motion vectors [ref:bevy-0.20.1-ray-query]. It holds no terrain, vegetation, wind, rain, vehicle or particle system; water is an example material of scrolled normal maps that supports deferred rendering only (`assets/shaders/water_material.wesl`) [ref:bevy-0.20.1-water-example]. Else: clustered decals that work in forward (`crates/bevy_pbr/src/decal/clustered.rs`) [ref:bevy-0.20.1-clustered-decals], an atmosphere whose functions carry a BSD-3-Clause header (`crates/bevy_pbr/src/atmosphere/bruneton_functions.wesl`), fog volumes (`crates/bevy_light/src/volumetric.rs`). Maturity: by its readme, early [ref:bevy-0.20.1-maturity].

**bevy_terrain** (`5a88daf`, 2024-07-17), MIT OR Apache-2.0 [ref:bevy-terrain-5a88daf-licence], source read; a plugin for Bevy 0.14 in WGSL. Tiles are refined in a compute prepass per view and drawn by one indirect draw, morphed by a scheme modified from CDLOD, over a chunked clipmap of streamed attachments; frustum culling reads a min-max height attachment (`docs/implementation.md`, `src/render/mod.rs`, `src/render/tiling_prepass.rs`, `src/shaders/tiling_prepass/refine_tiles.wgsl`, `src/shaders/render/vertex.wgsl`, `src/shaders/functions.wgsl`, `src/terrain_data/tile_tree.rs`, `src/terrain_data/tile_atlas.rs`) [ref:bevy-terrain-5a88daf-udlod]. It uses no tessellation stage. Its notes list splat texturing, shadows and collision as missing [ref:bevy-terrain-5a88daf-missing], and its readme calls it early development [ref:bevy-terrain-5a88daf-state].
